🎣 Fishing Spots

Sorted by distance from Willow Spring. All spots are bank-fishable unless noted.

Panther Lake (Private)

~5 mi
Largemouth Bass Catfish Bream Perch Chain Pickerel Bank Fishing Jon Boat Rental Heavy Vegetation
Closest spot to home. Private lake (Panther Lake LLC) with bank fishing and jon boat rentals. They hand you a map at check-in with a bunch of fishing locations, but most are boat-access only. Massively choked with vegetation. Forum anglers describe pulling up salad every cast, enough to entangle trolling motors and paddles if you try to cross the center. Giant fish reportedly in there, but the weed situation makes it rough. Pickerel anglers have luck with spinners and green pumpkin ned rigs worked through the edges.

On-Site Access Points

Main Fishing Pier Currently destroyed. Looks like a cannonball hit it. Not usable as of early 2026.
Second Pier Exists somewhere on the property. Couldn't locate it on first visit.
Pond Separate smaller pond on-site. Reportedly has trout. Fished it, didn't catch anything, but fish are supposedly in there. Okay for a casual try.

Bass Lake Park, Holly Springs

~8 mi
Bass Catfish (stocked) Crappie Free Pier
50-acre lake with a free fishing pier. Stocked with catfish. The nature center on-site sells bait. Great for a chill afternoon, and Bass Lake Park has a 2-mile walking trail around the lake if Bekah comes along. Easy, low-commitment trip.
Bluegill (Bream) 6", 2.02 oz. Off the fishing pier.

Lake Wheeler

~10 mi
Bass Catfish Crappie Trout (Simpkins Pond, stocked) No Bank Fishing on Lake
650-acre lake, 150 acres of parkland. No bank fishing allowed on the main lake shoreline. Fishing is restricted to two designated piers and the shoreline of Simpkins Pond. Boat ramp available if you ever get a kayak.

Where You Can Actually Fish

Simpkins Pond Bank fishing allowed here. NC Wildlife stocks it with trout (winter stocking, usually Dec-Jan). Also has bass and panfish. No boats allowed in the pond.
Accessible Fishing Dock East side of parking lot above the waterfront center. Sidewalk leads down to water's edge.
Second Fishing Pier City site lists two designated piers. Check at the park office for current status and location.
Bait stop: Lake Wheeler Grocery & Hardware (7330 Lake Wheeler Rd) on the way in. Live minnow tank and $5 nightcrawler containers. Rod and reel loaner program available at the park office (bait not included).

Shearon Harris Lake

~15 mi
Trophy Bass Crappie White Perch 16-20" Slot Limit
Nuclear plant cooling reservoir, so the water runs cleaner than Jordan. Trophy bass lake with a 16-20" protective slot limit on largemouth (can't keep fish between 16-20"). Forum reports say crappie bed right off the boat launch dock at the Harris ramp, about 20 feet out, catchable on jigs from shore. Smaller lake (4,100 acres) means less pressure dispersal. Good populations of largemouth, crappie, and white perch. Closer than Jordan if you don't want the drive.

Falls Lake

~25 mi
Bass Crappie Catfish Multiple Ramps
12,400 acres across 28 miles of Neuse River reservoir. Best variety of any local lake. The NC 50 bridge area has an underwater hump that holds crappie and bass, with the south end drop-off into the channel being the sweet spot. Multiple boat ramps and shore access points. Good all-around option if you want options.

Jordan Lake

~30 mi
Premier Bass Crappie Stripers Catfish Water Quality (Summer)
14,000 acres. NC's top trophy bass fishery. Feb-April offers the best shot at double-digit largemouth. 180+ miles of shoreline means plenty of bank access. Water quality issues from urban runoff cause summer algae blooms, but spring/fall/winter are fine. No specific consumption advisory beyond statewide mercury guidance (limit largemouth to ~once/week, panfish unlimited). This is where the big fish are.

Holly Shelter Game Land (Coastal)

~90 mi (Surf City area)
64,000 Acres Shooting Range Camping
Largest coastal game land in NC, near Surf City/Topsail. Not a regular fishing spot, but when you're working out-of-town jobs on the coast, the shooting range is nearby ($20 military discount, Wed-Sun). 100 and 275 yard outdoor rifle range. Good to know it's there.

🗺 Jordan Lake Trip Planner

The full route from Willow Spring: bait stop, primary spot, backup spot.

The Route

Stop 1: Buckhorn General Store (1208 W Academy St, Fuquay-Varina). Live minnows, worms, tackle, friendly staff. Right on your route heading west. Open til 6pm most days.

Stop 2: Beaver Creek Fishing Pier. Free, open 24hrs, parking right there. Pier over deeper water, crappie and panfish stack up on pier pilings. Primary spot.

Backup: Tailrace Fishing Area. Below the dam, 15 min south of Beaver Creek. Water from dam disorients baitfish, everything feeds. Stripers, catfish, panfish. Has restrooms.

Bank Fishing Strategy

Pre-spawn (Feb-April): South-facing banks that get afternoon sun warm fastest and pull bait in. Bass staging on first breaklines off spawning flats. Crappie on channel edges near brush. Jerkbaits (suspending, shad color) for bass. Minnows under slip bobber or 1/16oz white/chartreuse jigs for crappie.

Spawn (March-April): Crappie move into 8-12 ft range. This is prime time for bank anglers. 10-20+ fish days possible. Cast near any submerged wood, brush, or dock pilings.

Summer: Fish go deep. Tough from shore without a boat. Kayak changes everything.

📅 Seasonal Guide

When to go and what to target at Jordan Lake and nearby spots.

❄️

Winter (Dec-Feb)

SLOW

Crappie schooled 15-25 ft deep near structure. Bridge pilings are money. Fish midday when sun warms shallows. 2-6 fish sessions from bank. Tough without a boat.

🌸

Spring (Mar-May)

PRIME TIME

Pre-spawn bass move shallow Feb-Apr. Crappie spawn into 8-12 ft range. Best bank fishing of the year. 10-20+ fish days. South-facing sun-warmed banks first.

☀️

Summer (Jun-Aug)

TOUGH FROM SHORE

Fish go deep. Algae blooms at Jordan Lake. Early morning or late evening bites only. Kayak changes everything. Catfish bite year-round on cut bait.

🍂

Fall (Sep-Nov)

GOOD

Shad run triggers feeding frenzy. Bass push bait into shallows. Crappie re-school on structure. Second-best bank fishing window after spring.

💡 Quick Tips

Weather & Barometric Pressure

Falling barometer = fish feed aggressively. High stable pressure (30.3+) = "lockjaw", slow day. Best fishing: day before a storm, or warm front approaching with south winds. Worst: day after a cold front passes through.

Crappie Rig (Easy Mode)

Minnow on an Aberdeen hook, split shot 12" above, bobber set 3-4 ft deep. Cast near submerged wood, brush, or dock pilings. Wait for bobber to go under. Set hook GENTLY (papermouths). That's it. You're catching crappie.

License Info

NC Lifetime Sportsman License (disabled veteran discount). Covers all NC hunting and fishing. No annual renewal needed. When fishing at dad's area (SC), buy a 14-day non-resident license day-of when getting bait.

Kayak Upgrade Path

A sit-on-top fishing kayak ($200-400 used on Marketplace) is the biggest single upgrade for Jordan Lake. Gets you to structure bank anglers can't reach. Jordan is flat water, very kayak-friendly. Two solos > one tandem if Bekah comes. Check Marketplace for Perception Pescador Pro or Wilderness Systems. Trailer hauls them easy.

🌎 Grimesland, NC (Mom's Area)

Pitt County / Greenville area. Eastern NC coastal plain. Different fishery than the Triangle.

Grimesland Bridge Road Ponds (NCWRC)

Near Grimesland
Largemouth Bass Bream Bank Fishing NEW - Opened ~Fall 2024
Pitt County's first NCWRC-managed public fishing area. Shallow ponds on both sides of North Grimesland Bridge Road between NC-33 and US-264. 555-acre DOT property. Parking on both sides, kiosk with regs. Bank fishing only. Forum reports say bass hit chartreuse/black and yellow poppers. Can be hard to access on foot in some spots, canoe or jon boat would help. Watch for water moccasins.

Grimesland Sand Pits

Near Grimesland
Largemouth Bass Bream Bank Fishing Snakes
Old sand pits with multiple ponds. Forum anglers call it "bank basser heaven." The west pond that runs into the swamp reportedly holds the biggest fish. Hit or miss reports over the years. Healthy water moccasin population, so boots and awareness are a must. Forum tip: carry a gun due to occasional sketchy foot traffic in the area.

Tar River Access (Hwy 222)

Near Greenville
Catfish Shad Eel Wildlife Access Area
Coming out of Greenville on 5th Street, past ECU medical campus, over 264, continue to T intersection with 222. Turn right, access area is right before the bridge. Part of the Tar River. Water moves fast, so bottom fishing or weighted lures. Forum angler caught catfish and an eel under the bridge. Shad run is a big deal here in spring, bank anglers do well.

River Park North

Greenville
Bream Small Catfish Park Ponds
City park in Greenville with multiple ponds. Forum consensus: not great for bass. The second pond on the right produces bream and small catfish. Low expectations, but close to town if you just want to throw a line.

🌎 Elgin, SC (Dad's Area)

Kershaw County, South Carolina. Need a 14-day SC non-resident license (buy day-of when getting bait).

Lake Wateree

~20 mi from Elgin
Premier Fishery Largemouth Bass Crappie Striped Bass Catfish (Blue, Channel) Shellcrackers State Park + Bank
The big one near dad's place. 13,025 acres, 620 miles of shoreline, one of SC's oldest man-made lakes. SC DNR manages 17 fish attractors. Lake Wateree State Park (881 State Park Rd, Winnsboro) has bank fishing, a tackle loaner program, campground, boat ramp, and a nature trail. Crappie are famous here, not unusual to pull 2-pounders. Bass fishing is hot on the lower lake and near Beaver Creek. Catfish anglers should target Colonel Creek and Dutchman Creek in deeper water. The lake has a predominant shad forage base. Free public access at multiple ramps.

Goodale State Park

~10 mi from Elgin (Camden)
Bass Bream Bank + Kayak/Canoe
140-acre lake in Camden, just outside Elgin. Free admission. Boat and kayak rentals available. Also has a Civil War-era pond to explore. Quiet and low-key. Good for a casual day with dad.

Lake Murray

~45 mi from Elgin
Bass Crappie Striped Bass Catfish 11 Public Access Areas
50,000-acre monster lake with 650+ miles of shoreline. Farther drive from Elgin but worth it for a big fishing day. 11 public lake access areas. Multiple marinas. Serious bass and striper fishery. Only makes sense if you're making a day of it.

🌎 Sneads Ferry, NC (Coastal)

Saltwater fishing near Topsail Island. Different gear, different species, different game entirely.

New River Inlet

Sneads Ferry
Red Drum Flounder Speckled Trout Strong Current
Northern end of Topsail Island. Big drop-off with hard current, keep kids away from the water's edge. Don't cast far, drop bait in the trough next to the beach. Fish are right there. Good for reds and flounder. On the Sneads Ferry side of the high bridge, there's a spot you can fish out toward the bridge that produces flounder, black drum, and puppy reds.

Seaview Pier (North Topsail Beach)

Topsail Island
Spot Sea Mullet Croaker Red Drum Bluefish Fishing Pier
Classic NC ocean fishing pier at North Topsail Beach. Pier and surf anglers get spot, sea mullet, croakers, and red drum. Bluefish push through seasonally. Spanish mackerel and king mackerel in warmer months from the pier. This is your saltwater pier fishing experience.

Topsail Sound / ICW

Sneads Ferry area
Red Drum Flounder Bluefish Speckled Trout Shore + Kayak
The Intracoastal Waterway around Sneads Ferry has great inshore fishing from shore or kayak. Red drum around docks, grass banks, and shallow bays. Flounder on outgoing tides near structure. Speckled trout around inlet, bridges, and deep docks. A kayak here would be incredible for exploring the saltwater creeks (Charles Creek, Biglins Creek). Completely different fishing from the freshwater stuff at home.

Saltwater Fishing Notes

Your NC Lifetime Sportsman License covers saltwater too. Saltwater gear is different: heavier rod, braided line, circle hooks for drum. Cut bait (shrimp, mullet) for most inshore species. Best months are June-September for variety, but red drum bite year-round. Check NC DMF regs for slot limits on red drum and flounder seasons, they change annually.
