The Lookbook

Ten outfits in coastal sport prep. From court to clubhouse to regatta to terrace. Pastels, sailor colors, ribbons, polished loafers, and the leisure-class sport that ties them together.
Volume I · Edition One

A rich girl leaving tennis practice, going to the yacht club for lunch, then changing into an equestrian jacket later. All in pastels, navy, white, red, ribbons, pearls, and polished sport gear. That is the whole thesis. Not preppy in the school-uniform sense. Not yuppie in the corporate sense. Not coastal grandmother. Not quiet luxury beige minimalism. Something specific: women's country-club sportswear made bright, feminine, and a little tongue-in-cheek.

feminine prep + leisure sport + inherited-club symbolism + pastel and nautical palette

The clothes belong around tennis courts, sailboats, croquet lawns, club porches, regattas, derby brunches, golf fairways, and the quieter Palm Beach terraces. Built to layer, mix between sports, and travel across an afternoon without changing more than the shoes.

The ten looks below are organized by color and occasion, bright to soft. Each one carries the same grammar — pastel separates, polished footwear, club crest, ribbon or bow somewhere — and varies the accent.

Why it works.

i.
One color leads.
Each look picks a single pastel as the hero — banana, lilac, seafoam, coral, key lime, baby blue, soft pink — and lets every other piece serve it.
ii.
Navy is the spine.
A navy knit on the shoulder, a navy ribbon, a navy bag handle, a navy shutter behind. The grown-up anchor that keeps the pastel from going saccharine.
iii.
Always one bow.
Hair ribbon, sash, hat band, scarf knot, belt. Somewhere in every look there is a bow, even a small one. The signature.
iv.
Polished feet.
Bit loafers, ballet flats, boat shoes, slingbacks. Sneakers stay on the court. Off the court, leather and a buckle.
v.
A monogram somewhere.
Embroidered on the polo, stamped on the bag, etched on the loafer toe. Inherited-feeling even when freshly bought.
vi.
Crest at the top.
Tennis racquets, polo mallets, anchors, horseshoes. The club seal makes any outfit feel like it belongs to a place.
