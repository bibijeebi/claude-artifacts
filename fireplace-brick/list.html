Pro Tips Before You Go
  1. Confirm Carbon stock at Lowe's Garner before driving over. Call (919) 779-3370 or check the Lowe's app aisle locator after sign-in.
  2. Open the Providence Carbon carton in the aisle if they let you. Tumbled brick has natural variation, sometimes a box leans more brown than gray, sometimes the opposite. Pick the one that looks right with your cedar mantel — pull a phone photo of your fireplace before you go.
  3. Pro desk for the military discount. 10% off entire purchase with TAN DoD ID. Don't pay full price at self-checkout.
  4. Buy 4 tubes of PL Premium, not 3. Extra $8 to avoid a return trip Sunday morning when you're mid-install.
  5. If Carbon doesn't look right, alternates on the same shelf at the same ~$45 price: Alberta (warm red), Wind River (multi-tone reds and grays), Stillwater (light tan). Lowe's stocks the full Providence series.
