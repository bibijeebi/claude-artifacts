Fireplace Brick

One trip. One afternoon. Done.

Estimated Total $175–235
Two product paths. Pick ONE. Path A: Brickwebb mesh sheets ($115) — easier, pre-spaced bricks on a flexible mesh, faster install. Path B: Thin Brick Singles ($91) — same exact brick, sold as 50 loose bricks instead of mesh. $24 cheaper, more flexibility for a thin band, slightly slower since you space them by hand. For a 4″ trim band on this fireplace, either works. Pick singles if you want max savings; pick Brickwebb if you want max speed.

Pro Tips Before You Go
  1. Call ahead on the Brickwebb / Singles. Color and stock vary by store. Garner HD: (919) 779-9300. Confirm they actually have BW-37006CS or TB-27006CS in your color before driving over.
  2. Open the box at the store if they let you. Brickwebb color blends vary carton to carton, occasionally dramatically. Pick the one that looks right with your cedar mantel.
  3. Pro desk for the military discount. Don't pay full price at self-checkout. Show your TAN DoD ID at the pro desk for 10% off the whole receipt.
  4. Buy one EXTRA tube of PL Premium beyond what the list says. $8 worth of insurance against a Sunday morning second trip.
  5. Bring your tape measure. Measure your actual fireplace surround field one more time before committing to the brick color, just in case the area is bigger than estimated.
