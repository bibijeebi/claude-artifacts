Chuck Johnson

Charles C. Johnson — Clearview AI Co-Founder
b. 1988Dorchester, MAAge ~37Blogger / Entrepreneur

Charles C. Johnson is a right-wing provocateur, blogger, and entrepreneur who occupied a unique position at the intersection of the alt-right, Silicon Valley, and Trump-era Republican politics. He is the co-founder of Clearview AI, the controversial facial recognition company that scraped billions of photos from the internet.

Education & Early Career

Johnson grew up in Milton, Massachusetts, attending Milton Academy on scholarship (class of 2007), where classmates described him as "definitely a pariah." He attended Claremont McKenna College, created the Claremont Conservative blog, wrote two books (Encounter Books, 2013), and launched GotNews.com, a "news" site notorious for publishing wild, often false allegations.

Alt-Right Operations

Johnson ran three interconnected platforms: GotNews.com (news/conspiracy), WeSearchr.com (crowdfunding "bounties" for information), and FreeStartr.com (crowdfunding for deplatformed right-wing figures, raising legal defense money for Richard Spencer, Tommy Robinson, Faith Goldy, and others). FreeStartr was banned by Stripe and PayPal in 2018. GotNews brought in just $9,290 in 2017 and $1,412 in 2018 before ceasing publication.

Johnson appeared on Mike Enoch's Fash the Nation podcast in 2016, discussed "race realism," and claimed ethnic stereotypes are "largely true." He has questioned whether 6 million Jews died in the Holocaust.

The power broker: Johnson's most significant role was as an informal advisor during the Trump transition. Forbes confirmed he worked with transition executive committee members, including Peter Thiel, to recommend and vet appointees. He attended the 2018 State of the Union as a congressman's guest, met Trump's Interior Secretary about the border wall, and traveled to London to meet Julian Assange with Rep. Dana Rohrabacher. He was implicated in the Russia investigation through connections to efforts to locate Clinton's deleted emails.
Clearview AI & Legal Fallout

A 2021 New York Times Magazine investigation revealed Johnson as a co-founder of Clearview AI, the facial recognition company that built a surveillance tool used by law enforcement by scraping billions of photos from the internet without consent. Reporting also indicated Johnson was a federal informant. His family had intelligence services and US Navy connections.

Johnson was permanently banned from Twitter in 2015 for a tweet perceived as threatening against BLM activist DeRay McKesson. He was involved in shopping a forged document alleging misconduct by Senator Chuck Schumer (he and Mike Cernovich later claimed they were "duped"). In 2025, Johnson was found liable for civil racketeering and ordered to pay over $40 million to plaintiffs who accused him of falsely portraying himself as a US intelligence operative. GotNews went bankrupt after a separate defamation lawsuit over falsely linking a man to the Charlottesville murder.
