Recipe: Vanilla Ice Cream

This version uses 1 gallon of milk. No cream needed. Cream cheese adds the fat.

Main Ingredients

  • 4 cups raw milk
  • 4 oz cream cheese (half a Philadelphia brick, full-fat, plain)
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 4 egg yolks
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract (or 1 vanilla bean, scraped)
  • Pinch of salt

For the Bucket Maker

  • Rock salt / ice cream salt (~1 cup)
  • 10 lbs ice

Steps

  1. Whisk yolks + sugar until pale
  2. Heat milk to steaming (170°F) - don't boil
  3. Slowly pour hot milk into yolks while whisking (temper it)
  4. Return to pot, stir constantly until it coats a spoon (170-175°F)
  5. Cut heat, whisk in softened cream cheese until smooth
  6. Strain through mesh strainer, add vanilla + salt
  7. Chill in fridge 4+ hours (overnight = better texture)
  8. Pour into bucket maker canister
  9. Layer ice + rock salt in bucket (3:1 ratio ice to salt)
  10. Churn 20-30 min until thick
  11. Transfer to container, freeze 2+ hours for scoopable texture

If You Want Real Cream Instead

Buy 2 gallons. Let sit in fridge 12-24 hours undisturbed. Skim cream layer off top with ladle. Jersey milk yields ~1-1.5 cups cream per gallon.

With cream, use: 2 cups raw milk, 2 cups skimmed cream, 3/4 cup sugar, 4 egg yolks, 2 tsp vanilla, pinch of salt. Skip the cream cheese.

Butter Pecan Variation

Add to base recipe: 1 cup pecans, 2 tbsp butter, 1/4 tsp maple extract (optional)

Toast pecans in butter over medium heat until butter browns and nuts smell amazing. Salt them. Cool separately. Fold into ice cream in last 5 minutes of churning.

Notes

  • Jersey cows = higher butterfat (5-6%), ideal for ice cream
  • "Raw pet milk" = legal in NC, everyone knows what it is
  • Raw milk is unhomogenized, so cream rises to top naturally
  • Cooking the custard to 170°F handles any safety concerns
  • Don't over-churn or you'll make butter
  • Cold base before churning = better texture
