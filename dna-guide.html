You Only Need ONE DNA Test

Already done AncestryDNA, 23andMe, or any other test? Here's why you should never buy another one — and how to get everything you want for free.

Here's the Secret the DNA Companies Don't Advertise

Every consumer DNA test — whether it's AncestryDNA, 23andMe, MyHeritage, or any other — tests the exact same ~700,000 genetic markers. That's about 0.02% of your genome, but it's the same 0.02% everyone tests.

The difference between companies? Just the marketing, the website interface, and whose match database you can search. The actual genetic data you get is identical.

Once you download your raw data from any test, you can run all the same analyses these companies offer — usually for free.

What Happens When You Buy Multiple Tests

❌ Buying Another Test ($99-199)

  • Same 700,000 genetic markers you already have
  • Different ethnicity pie chart (same data, different algorithm)
  • Health reports you can generate free
  • Access to one more match database

✓ Using Your Existing Data (Free-$12)

  • Upload to GEDmatch — search multiple databases at once
  • Run Promethease — comprehensive health report ($12)
  • Try Genetic Genie — methylation analysis (free)
  • Explore Genomelink — 100+ trait reports (free tier)

Every additional test is paying for data you already own.

What To Do Instead (4 Simple Steps)

1

Download Your Raw Data

Log into your existing DNA testing account (Ancestry, 23andMe, etc.). Go to Settings, find "Download Raw DNA Data," and save the file to your computer. It's a small text file — think of it as YOUR genetic blueprint that YOU own.

2

Back It Up Somewhere Safe

Save a copy on a USB drive or cloud storage. Companies can shut down, change policies, or remove download options. Once you have the file, it's yours forever.

3

Upload to Free Analysis Sites

Take that same file and upload it to sites like GEDmatch (relative matching), Genetic Genie (health), or Genomelink (traits). Same data, different insights — all free or nearly free.

4

Stop Buying More Tests

Seriously. Unless you specifically need access to a proprietary match database (betting your unknown relatives ONLY use that one site), you're just buying the same genetic data with different packaging.

Free & Cheap Tools That Accept Your Data

GEDmatch

Free

~$12 one-time

Comprehensive health report based on published research. Covers thousands of genetic variants linked to health conditions and traits.

Genetic Genie

Free

Methylation and detox pathway analysis. Useful for understanding how your body processes nutrients and toxins.

Genomelink

Free tier available

100+ trait reports including ancient ancestry, personality insights, and nutrition recommendations.

DNA.Land

Free

Biogeographical analysis, imputation (fills in gaps in your data), and relative matching.

FamilyTreeDNA

Free transfer

Upload your data for free and get access to their match database and analysis tools.

The Bottom Line

Your DNA was sequenced. The data exists. Everything else is just software interpreting the same ~700,000 data points. Don't pay for it twice.
