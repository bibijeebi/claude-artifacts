Race 1: Charlottesville Half Marathon

Charlottesville Half Marathon
Saturday, April 11, 2026 — Charlottesville, VA — ~4 hr drive

25th anniversary. Boutique race, small field, no massive crowds. The course runs along the Rivanna River through historic Charlottesville neighborhoods, past horse farms and vineyards, with views of the Blue Ridge Mountains. It's about 65% roads and 35% paved bike paths. The course is hilly. They're upfront about it. This is not a flat PR course, it's a scenic one.

Runner's World called it one of the top ten best new marathons in the U.S., and the New York Times put it in their top five destination marathons. The start and finish are in downtown Charlottesville, which has good restaurants, breweries, and a pedestrian mall. Monticello is 15 minutes away. Wineries everywhere.

Open to traffic. No headphones recommended (you need to hear cars). No strollers, no pets. Water stops at miles 2, 3.5, 7.1, 8.7, 9.8, and 11.1 with Gatorade and gels.

Start Time 7:00 AM
Time Limit 3.5 hours (16 min/mi)
Entry Fee $75+ per person
Registration Closes April 9 online (on-site available)
Packet Pickup Fri 2-6 PM or Sat 5:45-6:45 AM
Course Hilly, scenic, open to traffic
The weekend plan: Drive up Friday afternoon (~4 hrs). Pick up packets Friday 2-6 PM at Market St Park. Explore downtown Charlottesville, hit a brewery or winery. Race Saturday 7 AM. Done by 10:30 at the latest. Grab lunch, drive home, or stick around another night if we want.

Race 2: Outer Banks Marathon

Outer Banks TowneBank Marathon
Saturday, November 7, 2026 — Manteo / Nags Head, NC — ~4 hr drive — 25th Anniversary

The oldest continuously running road race in the Outer Banks. Fast and flat course that crosses the barrier islands with ocean views, sand dunes, and natural scenery. 2026 is the 25th anniversary, so they're going big. Post-race includes free southern BBQ for all runners, live music, and a beer garden.

Also has a half marathon, 8K, 5K, and Color Fun Run that weekend, so if anyone else wants to come do a shorter distance they can. Open registration, no lottery, no qualification required. This would be our first full marathon (26.2 miles).

Distance 26.2 miles (full marathon)
Time Limit ~6 hours (est.)
Entry Fee ~$80-120 per person (tiered)
Course Fast, flat, barrier island roads
Post-Race Free BBQ, beer garden, live music
Weekend Events Marathon, Half, 8K, 5K, Fun Run
The weekend plan: Drive to OBX Friday (~4 hrs). Get a rental for the weekend. Packet pickup / expo Saturday. Race Sunday morning. Free BBQ and beer after. Hang out at the beach if weather cooperates. Drive home Monday or Sunday evening. Could also invite Rachel, Clifford, or whoever to do the half or 5K.

Why these two

The Charlottesville half gives us a race under our belts early in the year and proves we can do 13.1 on a legit hilly course. It's a different experience from the Historic Half we did last year. Smaller, quieter, more scenic, and a good excuse for a weekend trip to a cool town.

The OBX full is flat, fast, beginner-friendly, and close enough to drive. The 25th anniversary means they'll be going all out. November weather on the coast is usually mid-50s to low-60s, which is about as good as it gets for marathon conditions. And free BBQ at the finish is a strong closer.

The spacing works too. April half gives us nearly 7 months to train for the full, which is more than enough time. Standard marathon training plans are 16-20 weeks, so we'd have room to ease into it.
