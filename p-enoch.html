Mike Enoch (real name Michael Peinovich) is the founder of The Right Stuff (TRS), a neo-Nazi media network, and co-host of The Daily Shoah, one of the most influential podcasts of the alt-right era. He is a former software developer from New York's Upper East Side.

The Right Stuff & Daily Shoah

TRS launched in 2012 and evolved into a podcast network. The Daily Shoah (a pun on The Daily Show using the Hebrew word for the Holocaust) debuted August 3, 2014 with a rotating "death panel" of hosts. The show became notorious for popularizing the (((triple parentheses))) "echo" notation used to identify Jewish people online, now recognized by the ADL as a hate symbol. At peak, TRS claimed approximately 100,000 weekly listeners.

The Doxxing (January 2017): 8chan users leaked Peinovich's identity, revealing this leading neo-Nazi propagandist was: (a) a Manhattan software developer, (b) from a suburban New Jersey family, and most devastatingly, (c) married to a Jewish woman whose wedding featured traditional Jewish rites. His wife had even appeared on The Daily Shoah performing a neo-Nazi parody. Peinovich admitted: "Yes my wife is who they say she is." They separated mid-2017 and divorced.
Post-Doxxing Career

Despite the scandal, Peinovich returned to TRS and became more publicly active. He spoke at Unite the Right in Charlottesville and was listed as a defendant in Sines v. Kessler (ultimately dismissed). In 2020, he co-founded the National Justice Party (NJP), promoting "white civil rights" and economic nationalism. After the October 2023 Hamas attack, Peinovich wrote: "Hats off to the Palestinians for taking bold and courageous action."

He lost his software development job after being outed and was identified in his neighborhood via flyers. He promotes white nationalism, antisemitism, Holocaust denial, and the white genocide conspiracy theory.
