Friction-hitch diameter rule (the big paracord trap)

Prusik, klemheist, VT, autoblock, distel, schwabisch only grip if the hitch cord is thinner than the host (~60-80%). Same-diameter paracord on paracord slides off under load. The honest exception is Blake's, tied in the line itself, same cord, holds. That's your one true single-paracord friction hitch.

Square knot is a binding, not a bend

Loaded as a join it spills, worse on slick paracord. To connect two ropes use a sheet bend, fisherman's, or zeppelin.

The collisions you half-caught

Fisherman's KNOT (the line-joiner you know) vs Ashley's Fisherman's BEND (an anchor hitch) — both in the tree, tagged. Your "figure 9" is the directional/inline figure-8 loop. Asher's Equalizer is real, the jug-sling handle. Magnus hitch is a rolling hitch with the last hitch reversed, and you already tie the rolling hitch every time you set a taut-line.

Paracord cordcraft: gut it

Pull the inner yarns and the hollow sheath splices like hollow braid (bury / Brummel / the Manny join). Two-strand deco (lanyard, snake, cobra) comes off one folded cord; multi-strand (crown, Matthew Walker) needs several cords or gutted yarns.
