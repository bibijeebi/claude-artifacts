02

Your tower's API is mine. I can grab YouTube transcripts, download videos with yt-dlp, run reverse image search, browse the 6TB drive, fire shell commands, all without you touching the desktop.

03

Slip past anti-bot defenses

The smart-scraper worker runs a real browser with your session cookies preloaded. JS-only pages, Cloudflare-gated sites, Facebook content, all readable. Stock web_fetch can't see any of that.

04

FB Marketplace → browsable site

Drop a search URL. I scrape listings, screenshot each one cropped to the panel, save as .webp, push to R2, and deploy a single page with direct links to every listing. You just scroll.

05

Generate images mid-build

Gemini imagegen via the deploy worker (async, fire-and-forget, R2-backed). I generate hero art, OG cards, illustrations, then crop, compress, upload, and embed them, all inside the same response. OpenAI / Anthropic keys also loaded.

06

One-shot deploys to your domain

HTML, JS, CSS, binary assets, OG meta, R2-hosted images, custom worker routes. The deploy pipeline turns "make me a page about X" into a public URL in under a minute.

07

Recall any past conversation

Semantic + keyword search over your full chat history (6,000+ conversations). "What did we decide about X six months ago" actually works. Pulls the relevant turn back into context.

08

Live-write to your Anki deck

The Anki MCP tunnels into AnkiConnect on your Mac. I add cards, query reviews, suspend, search, all against your real deck, while we're talking.

09

Full WGU pipeline

Slurp pulls course content. Cram banks generate from it. The OA tutor preps you for objective assessments. The ghostwriter completes assignments in your voice and inspects templates for AI honeypots before submitting.

10

Animated reveal.js decks

Auto-animate transitions, fragment reveals, syntax-highlighted code, speaker notes, custom themes. Deployable as a public URL. Screen-record the result and you have a video.

11

Web-playable games

HTML5 canvas, three.js, p5, plain JS. Built single-file, deployed to a URL you can share. Browser-only, no install.

12

Real LaTeX documents

Full preamble, packages, math, FontAwesome icons, the works. .tex source only, you compile with latexmk -pdf (compiling here hangs the chat).
