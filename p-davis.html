Joel Davis

National Socialist Network (Australia)
b. ~1994-1995AustraliaAge 30Neo-Nazi Propagandist

Joel Davis is a prominent Australian neo-Nazi and key figure in the National Socialist Network (NSN), Australia's most visible white supremacist organization, who gained international attention after his arrest in November 2025.

The NSN

The NSN formed in 2020 from two predecessor organizations (the Lads Society and Antipodean Resistance), led by Thomas Sewell, a convicted criminal and former Australian soldier. The NSN was active across all six Australian state capitals. Davis served as one of its highest-profile public figures, frequently livestreaming, posting ideological content, and speaking at events as the primary Sydney-based propagandist.

The Allegra Spender Incident

On November 8, 2025, approximately 60 NSN members rallied outside NSW Parliament in Sydney, chanting "abolish the Jewish lobby" and "blood and honour" (a Hitler Youth slogan). When independent federal MP Allegra Spender condemned the rally, Davis allegedly posted on encrypted Telegram calling supporters to "rhetorically rape" the parliamentarian.

Arrest & legal defense: Australian Federal Police arrested Davis in Bondi on November 20, 2025, charging him with using a carriage service to menace, harass, or offend. He was denied bail. His lawyer argued that "rhetorically rape" was "a philosophical term of art connoting robust debate," not a literal threat.
Aftermath

The NSN announced voluntary dissolution in January 2026 to avoid prosecution under new hate speech legislation (the Antisemitism, Hate and Extremism Act 2026, triggered by the Bondi massacre). Davis attempted to distance himself from the NSN in his bail application after this. He remained behind bars on remand as of early 2026.

Before his arrest, Davis had revealed plans for the NSN to register as a "White Australia" political party in NSW. In September 2025, he appeared inside NSW Parliament House at a Western Heritage Australia event, asking about "the advantages of racism and supremacy" during Q&A.
