📖 The Pattern

Spanish verbs end in -ar, -er, or -ir. To conjugate, drop the ending to get the stem, then add the personal ending for the subject.

Pro tip: The endings are nearly identical between -er and -ir. The only difference is in nosotros (-emos vs -imos) and vosotros (which we don't use anyway). Remember the -er/-ir pattern as one rule.
