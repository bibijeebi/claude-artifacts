Numbers, Dates & Time

Counting, telling time, and writing dates the Spanish way.

Numbers Ordinals Time Dates Drills Reading

📖 The Concept

Cardinal numbers (uno, dos, tres) count things. Ordinal numbers (primero, segundo) rank them.

Time: Es la una for 1 o'clock, Son las... for everything else. Add y media for half past, y cuarto for quarter past.

Dates are written day-month-year. The first of the month uses the ordinal primero; all other days use cardinal numbers (el dos de mayo, el quince de octubre).

Pro tip: Spanish uses periods for thousands (1.000) and commas for decimals (3,14). Opposite of English.
