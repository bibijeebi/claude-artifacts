Estar, Ser & Subject Pronouns

Spanish has two verbs for "to be." Pick the wrong one and you'll change the meaning. Here's how to never mix them up.

Pronouns Conjugation Drill Ser vs Estar Reading

📖 The Concept

Subject pronouns (yo, tú, él…) tell you who's doing the action. Spanish often drops them because the verb ending already says who's acting — but you need to know them cold.

Spanish splits English's "to be" into two verbs:

SER
Permanent or defining traits.
DOCTOR mnemonic:
Description · Occupation · Characteristic · Origin · Time · Relationship
ESTAR
Temporary states or location.
PLACE mnemonic:
Position · Location · Action (progressive) · Condition · Emotion

Pro tip: The same adjective can change meaning. Es aburrido = he is boring (trait). Está aburrido = he is bored (state). The verb does the work.
