📖 The Concept

Hay means both "there is" and "there are." It never changes. Hay un libro / Hay tres libros.

Question words always carry an accent (¿qué? not que). They go at the start of the question, after the inverted ¿.

Days, months, and seasons are written in lowercase in Spanish (lunes, enero, el verano), unlike English.
