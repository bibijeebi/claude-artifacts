Rifle Upgrade Guide

Benny's Sight Upgrade for the 511

A quick guide to the rear sight options I'm looking at for my Remington 511 Scoremaster .22 rifle.

What's the Deal?

The factory rear sight on my Remington 511 is a simple stamped-steel elevator sight that sits on a 3/8" dovetail cut into the barrel. It only adjusts for elevation (up/down) in rough steps, and you can't adjust windage (left/right) at all without drifting the whole thing with a punch. It's not great.

I want to replace it with something that has proper adjustments for both windage and elevation, and ideally fiber optic inserts so it's easier to see in low light (dawn and dusk when squirrels are most active).

All three options below drop right into the same 3/8" dovetail slot on the barrel. No drilling, no gunsmithing, just remove two screws, slide the old sight out, slide the new one in.

I'm leaning toward the HIVIZ UNI2006, about $21, fully adjustable for both windage and elevation, has fiber optics, and fits right in. Best bang for the buck. The Williams is nicer but costs twice as much for the same job.

What About a Scope?

My 511 doesn't have a grooved receiver or factory scope mounting holes, which makes scoping it a bit of a project. Here are the realistic options:

Option A: Drill & Tap the Receiver

A gunsmith drills and taps holes into the receiver for Weaver #43 bases. Costs about $30-50 for the labor plus bases and rings. Permanent modification, but gives a solid mount.

Option B: Weaver Side Mount (eBay)

Old-school Weaver N-series side mounts were made for the 510/511/512 series. They bolt to the left side of the receiver using the existing rear sight screw holes. No drilling. Find them on eBay for $20-40.

Option C: Dovetail Adapter Rail

A 3/8" dovetail-to-Picatinny adapter could sit in the barrel dovetail, but it's only one mounting point, positioned far forward. Bad eye relief, wobbly. Not recommended.

For now I'm sticking with iron sights. This is a backyard squirrel rifle shooting .22 CB shorts at 50 feet, not a precision target gun. A good adjustable rear sight is all I really need.

Bottom Line

I'm probably going with the HIVIZ UNI2006 for about $21. Full windage and elevation adjustment with fiber optics, drops right into the existing dovetail, and it's the best value of the three. Easy install, no gunsmith needed.
