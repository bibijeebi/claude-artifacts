Nic tolerance baseline

Heavy vape/pouch history (Juul menthol, gas station disposables, pouches, gum). Cigars physically can't match vape nic delivery because you don't inhale, nic absorbs through mouth lining over 45–90 min. "Full body" ≠ "more nic punch," it means more intense FLAVOR. Stop chasing nic, chase flavor.

Storage rules

  • Target 65–68% RH. 72%+ = hard to light, muted flavor.
  • Boveda 69% packs + sealed container (tupperdor) works fine.
  • Rest sticks 1–2 weeks after purchase before smoking (shop humidity often high).
  • Exception: wax-sealed tubes (Gurkha Bourbon etc) don't need resting.
