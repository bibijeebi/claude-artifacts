Pro tips

  1. Submit Form 816 every semester after registering. The Wake Tech online form at waketech.edu/student-services/military-veterans/education-benefits/apply is what triggers the SCO to certify your enrollment to VA. No appointment needed.
  2. Monthly verification at VA.gov (or via text after opting in). Required since Jan 2026. Miss two months = housing payments paused.
  3. Capt. Binns is the strategic pick. 25-year Garner PD veteran, FBI National Academy graduate, state-certified instructor since 2003. Sit front row, talk to him after class about your LE goals. He's a Wake County LE network in one person.
  4. Petition MAT-143 substitution. You already finished MAT-263 (Brief Calc) with a B. Email CJC dept chair + registrar requesting MAT-263 sub for MAT-143. Saves a whole class.
  5. Summer 2026 BAH math: 9 credits in a 10-week summer term puts you above 50% rate of pursuit, so MHA flows. Binns being hybrid unlocks the in-person rate. Expect roughly $1,500-1,800/mo for the term.
  6. Register early. In-person CJC sections fill within hours, especially CJC-121, CJC-221, and CJC-225. Calendar alarm the day registration opens.
  7. Hybrid = in-person for BAH. If a class meets even once a month physically, it qualifies. Binns' section is the textbook example.
  8. Sequencing: CJC-131 (summer) before CJC-231 (fall) ✓. CJC-111 first ✓. CJC-255 capstone always last ✓. CJC-221 before CJC-120 ✓.
  9. Most CJC classes run at PSEC (Public Safety Education Campus) or Southern Wake. Both closer to Willow Spring than Northern Wake.
  10. WAVE office (Wake Tech vets) for any VA red tape. Building H, main campus. Email-first if you don't want to drive in.
