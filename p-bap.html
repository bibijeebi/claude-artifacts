Bronze Age Pervert

Costin Vlad Alamariu
b. May 21, 1980Bucharest, RomaniaAge 45Philosopher / Author

Bronze Age Pervert (BAP) is the pseudonymous persona of Costin Vlad Alamariu, a Romanian-American political theorist who has become perhaps the most intellectually influential figure on the online dissident right, operating at the intersection of academic philosophy, bodybuilding culture, and Nietzschean vitalism.

Academic Pedigree

Alamariu immigrated to the US at age 10, growing up in Newton, Massachusetts. His credentials are exceptional: mathematics at MIT, philosophy MA at Columbia, PhD in political science from Yale (2015). His dissertation was titled "The Problem of Tyranny and Philosophy in the Thought of Plato and Nietzsche." He completed a postdoctoral fellowship at Emory University (2015-2016).

The Persona

The BAP character first appeared on forums around 2010, then Twitter in 2013 (@bronzeageperv, banned 2017, reinstated 2023). It is deliberately outlandish: a self-described "Steppe barbarian. Nationalist, Fascist, Nudist Bodybuilder" who writes in an affected "caveman patois" full of deliberate misspellings. The ironic veneer coats genuinely radical philosophical positions.

Key Works

Bronze Age Mindset (2018, self-published) became an underground sensation celebrating physical vitality, ancient warrior aristocracy, and rejection of democratic egalitarianism. It drew enough attention that Claremont Institute's Michael Anton wrote an unsolicited review. In September 2023, Selective Breeding and the Birth of Philosophy (his reworked Yale dissertation) briefly cracked Amazon's top 25 bestsellers. It argues philosophy originated in eugenic breeding practices among ancient Greek elites, that "virtue cannot be taught" and is "a matter of blood." His dissertation advisor reportedly called him a Nazi.

Core thesis: BAP advocates rebreeding "the original Aryan race, or as close an approximation as possible, through some kind of Platonic Lebensborn program." He views women's liberation as "an infection from which the West can't recover without the most terrible convulsions." He has expressed contempt for both the American Founding and Christianity. He praised Israel as "national socialist through and through."

BAP's reach extends well beyond the fringe: discussed by Tyler Cowen, Julius Krein, The Atlantic, and First Things. Peter Thiel and Curtis Yarvin have promoted his content. He reportedly spends much time in Latin America.
