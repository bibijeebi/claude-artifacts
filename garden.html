🫐 Blueberries: All Good

Normal dormancy. Bare stems + clinging leaves = fine for December.

🌿 Will leaf out in spring

🌿 Fruit comes in summer

Now: Optional cleanup of dead branches

Feb: Real pruning before buds swell

🍅 Tomato: It's Done

Cracked + black gash = game over

🚫 Don't eat that tomato. Blossom end rot + splitting.

1. Yank the whole plant + cage

2. Toss in compost or trash

3. Refresh soil for next year

4. Replant in April-May

Tomatoes are annuals in NC. This is the cycle.

Yeah, It's Cooked

Dead foliage, desiccated stems. No saving it.
