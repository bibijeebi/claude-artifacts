McGill Big 3 — Spine Protection Core

Do every strength day. Order matters.

  1. McGill Curl-Up: 3 × 5 reps, 10-sec holds. Lie on back, one knee bent, one straight. Hands flat UNDER low back. Lift head/shoulders 1 inch only. Chin tucked.
  2. Side Plank: 3 × 15-30 sec each side. Body straight from head to heels. Drop bottom knee if needed.
  3. Bird Dog: 3 × 5 each side, 10-sec hold. All fours, neutral spine. Opposite arm + leg extends slowly. Draw a small square at return.
  4. Dead Bug (bonus): 3 × 10 each side. On back, knees over hips, arms up. Lower opposite arm/leg without arching low back.

Push-Up Form Checklist

  1. Hands shoulder-width, fingers forward
  2. Elbows track ~45° from torso (NOT flared 90°)
  3. Chest to floor at bottom (not nose, not belly)
  4. Body in plank — no sagging hips, no piked butt
  5. Full lockout at top
  6. Better to do 5 perfect than 15 sloppy

GTG Rules

  1. Test max every 2 weeks, update daily rep number
  2. Daily rep = 40-50% of max, rounded down
  3. 5 sets per day, spaced 30+ min apart
  4. NEVER go to failure on GTG sets
  5. Form perfect every rep — sloppy reps train sloppy patterns
  6. On max test days, skip GTG (max effort needs recovery)
  7. Sick/traveling? 1 set of 1 rep counts. Streak matters.

Running Rules

  1. 80% of runs at conversational pace — can talk in full sentences
  2. 20% at tempo or threshold (Phase 2+)
  3. SOLE F80 treadmill at 1-2% incline simulates outdoor running
  4. If you feel pain (sharp, not sore): STOP. Walk home.
  5. Goal: 5 miles @ 9:00 pace by week 32
  6. Don't time runs in Phase 1. Just finish them.

Recovery Non-Negotiables

  1. 8 hours of sleep minimum. Recovery happens here.
  2. 100-125g protein/day (your bodyweight in grams)
  3. Hydrate: half your bodyweight in ounces of water
  4. 1 full rest day per week minimum (no cardio, no lifting)
  5. Soreness = fine. Sharp pain = injury — see someone.
  6. If exhausted 3+ days in a row, take an extra rest day

Phase Goals at a Glance

  1. Phase 1 — BASE (wk 1-8): 40 min easy run continuous. 15 push-ups max. Bulletproof spine.
  2. Phase 2 — BUILD (wk 9-16): 5 mi continuous. 25 push-ups max. Real strength (squat, deadlift, bench, pull-up). Add rucking.
  3. Phase 3 — SPECIFICITY (wk 17-24): 5 mi at 10:00. 35 push-ups max. Academy-style circuits. 25-lb rucks.
  4. Phase 4 — PEAK (wk 25-32): 5 mi at 9:00. 50 push-ups max. Taper last 2 weeks. Arrive fresh.
