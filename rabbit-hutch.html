Overview

A simple, sturdy indoor hutch built from 2×2 lumber and hardware cloth, designed to sit in a living room corner. Two walls become plywood (against the walls), the rest is wire mesh for airflow and visibility. Divided into two equal cells with separate door access.

Build Steps

  1. Cut all 2×2s to length. Four at 60", eleven at 24". Use a miter saw or circular saw.
  2. Assemble the bottom frame. Two 60" rails + two 24" end pieces form a 2'×5' rectangle. Pre-drill and screw at each corner.
  3. Assemble the top frame. Same thing: two 60" + two 24". Identical rectangle.
  4. Connect top and bottom with corner posts. Stand four 24" verticals at each corner, screw into both frames. Now you have a box skeleton.
  5. Add center divider. Two 24" verticals + one 24" top rail at the midpoint (30" from each end). This separates the cells.
  6. Attach plywood panels. Screw or staple the back wall, short end wall, and top panel to the frame from the inside face.
  7. Attach wire mesh. Staple hardware cloth to the floor, open short end, front face (leaving door openings), and center divider.
  8. Build doors. Cut two rectangular wire panels to fit the front openings (~29"×20" each). Frame edges with scrap 2×2 if desired, or just fold wire edges for rigidity.
  9. Hang doors. Attach T-hinges at top of each door opening. Add barrel bolt latches at the side.
  10. Add drip tray. Cut leftover plywood to 24"×60", slide under the wire floor to catch waste. Optional: line with plastic sheeting for easy cleanup.
