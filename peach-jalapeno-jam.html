Peach Jalapeño Jam Sweet heat in a jar. Strawberry Hill Farms inspired.

The one that started it all. Cream cheese, cracker, spoonful of this. You already know.

Heat Level
SweetWarmSpicyFire
With 4-6 jalapeños (seeds removed from most) it's a warm tingle. Add 1 habanero and it goes from "jam with a kick" to "jam with authority." Your call.
🧊
Using frozen peaches: Thaw them completely and drain off excess liquid before measuring. Pat them dry-ish with paper towels. Frozen peaches are picked ripe and work great for jam. No need to peel or blanch since they're already skinless.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups peaches, diced smallFrozen: thaw and drain. Fresh: peel first (blanch 60sec, ice bath, skins slide off).
  • 4-6 jalapeños, seeded and minced fineLeave the seeds IN on 2 of them for extra heat.
  • 1 (optional) habanero, mincedFor real kick. Recommended. Wear gloves.
  • 6.5 cups sugarYes, this is a lot. It's jam. This is what makes it set.
  • 1/4 cup lemon juiceVolcano bottled lemon juice works perfectly for this.
  • 1 pouch liquid pectinIf you have powdered pectin, follow the box instructions instead. Timing is different.

Steps

1
Prep the peaches
Frozen: Thaw completely, drain off the liquid, pat dry with paper towels. Dice into small pieces, roughly 1/4 inch. Mash about half of them roughly with a potato masher or fork. You want some chunks and some mush.
Fresh: Score an X on the bottom of each peach. Blanch in boiling water for 60 seconds. Ice bath. Skins slide right off. Then dice and mash same as above.
2
Mince the peppers
Cut jalapeños in half. Remove seeds and ribs from most of them (leave seeds in 2 for heat). Mince as fine as you can. If using the habanero, mince that too.
Gloves on for the habanero. Jalapeños are less dangerous but still wash your hands after.
3
Combine peaches, peppers, sugar, and lemon juice
Dump it all into the biggest pot you have. Stir everything together really well. The sugar will look like way too much. It is. But that's how jam works.
4
Bring to a hard rolling boil
High heat, stirring constantly. A "rolling boil" means it keeps boiling even while you're stirring. This takes 5-10 minutes. Don't walk away, it'll boil over and make a biblical mess.
This is boiling sugar. It is insanely hot. Do not splash it on yourself. Use a long spoon.
5
Add pectin
Once you're at a hard rolling boil, squeeze in the entire pouch of liquid pectin. Stir it in immediately. Return to a hard rolling boil and boil for exactly 1 minute. Set a timer.
This is the critical step. Too little boil time = runny jam. Too much = rubber.
6
Remove from heat and skim
Take the pot off the burner. There'll be foam on top. Skim it off with a spoon and toss it. The foam is just air bubbles, it won't hurt anything but it looks ugly in the jar.
7
Fill the jars
Ladle hot jam into clean jars, leaving about 1/4 inch of space at the top. Wipe the jar rims clean with a damp paper towel (any jam on the rim = bad seal). Put the lids on finger-tight.
8
Water bath can (for shelf-stable)
Put filled jars in a pot of boiling water. Water should cover the jars by at least 1 inch. Boil for 10 minutes. Pull them out and set on a towel on the counter. Don't touch them. You'll hear the lids pop as they seal (that's the good sound).
Skip this step if you're just keeping it in the fridge. Fridge jam lasts 3-4 weeks. Canned jam lasts months.
9
Wait
Let jars cool completely at room temperature. Don't move them for at least 12 hours. The jam sets as it cools. The heat from the peppers also builds and distributes over the first 24 hours. It'll be better tomorrow.

🎁

Half-pint jars with a label. People lose their minds.

Pro Tips

⏰ Heat builds over 24 hours. Don't add more peppers to the recipe because it tastes mild when hot. Taste it tomorrow before you adjust your next batch.
🍑 Frozen peaches are fine. They're picked ripe and flash frozen, so you get real peach flavor year-round. Better than unripe grocery store peaches in February.
⚖️ Sugar is non-negotiable. Pectin needs sugar to gel. If you cut it, you get syrup not jam. If you want less sugar, get low-sugar pectin and follow those instructions instead.
🧪 Gel test: Put a plate in the freezer before you start. When you think the jam is done, drop a spoonful on the cold plate. Wait 30 sec. Push it with your finger. If it wrinkles, it's set. If it runs, boil longer.
📏 Check your sugar first. One batch eats 6.5 cups. That's almost a whole 5lb bag. Make sure you have enough before you start.
