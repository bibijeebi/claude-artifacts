The Setup

How it all comes together on your SOLE F80.

🎯 Zone 2 Cardio + Work

Walk at 3.0-3.5 mph, 1-3% incline. Laptop on VIVO desk, Wispr Flow active, Shokz on ears. Dictate to Claude, write emails, browse. Burn ~300 cal/hr without breaking a sweat. This is where the magic happens.

📺 Entertainment Mode

Bump speed to 3.5-4.5 mph. K400 controls the TV, phone in the mount for texts. Watch a show, documentary, or YouTube rabbit hole. Time evaporates. Remove the laptop desk if you're going above 4 mph.

🏃 Running Mode

Remove the laptop desk entirely. Shokz playing your playlist, phone in the mount for music control. The F80 handles up to 12 mph and 15% incline. No accessories in the way, just you and the belt.

🎓 Treadmill University

WGU coursework on the laptop while walking. Wispr Flow for taking notes by voice. Lectures on the TV via screen share. You're getting your steps AND your degree at the same time. GI Bill funding a walking education.
