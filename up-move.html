Why Michigan

Wisconsin was the first idea. Michigan is the better deal, and it isn't close.

Bottom line. Michigan fully exempts the homestead of a 100% P&T or IU-rated veteran from property tax. No value cap, no income test, no residency waiting period, and it covers the whole parcel the house sits on, farm use included. Wisconsin only refunds the tax on one acre, and only after you've lived there five years unless you enlisted from Wisconsin.

The exemption

MCL 211.7b. File Form 5107 with the township assessor by Dec 31 of the tax year. Starting 2026 you file once, not annually. Qualifies: 100% P&T entitled to the 100% rate, a VA SAH certificate, or rated individually unemployable. Bekah qualifies on her own the day TDIU is granted.

All or nothing: the property either gets a 100% exemption or none.

The two gotchas

One parcel. The exemption covers the homestead parcel only. House on one deed and 80 acres on another means the 80 gets taxed. Combine parcels before you file.

No business use. Any commercial use other than farming kills the whole exemption. Cattle, hay, timber, chickens: fine. Running a duct-cleaning shop out of the pole barn: not fine.

Special assessments (road, drain, township fire millage) still get paid.

The stack on top

Free DV plate at 100%/IU: no plate fee, no registration, no renewal. DV plates at 50%+ since 2025, so Bekah gets one now. Free non-lottery hunting and fishing licenses (deer, antlerless, turkey leftovers, waterfowl, small game, fur harvester). A DV-plated vehicle skips the Recreation Passport, so every state park, forest campground and boat launch is free. Children of Veterans Tuition Grant up to $2,800/yr for kids 16 to 26.

The cons, ranked by how much they'll bite

Taxes are a wash with NC (flat 4.25% income, 6% sales, VA comp untaxed everywhere). Detroit is 8 hours away and irrelevant. These are the real ones.

  1. Healthcare distance. Marquette has the one real hospital in the UP (UP Health System). Escanaba has OSF St. Francis. VA is Iron Mountain VAMC plus small clinics; anything specialized is Milwaukee or Ann Arbor. If Mom follows, we're picking her hospital too.
  2. Winter is a job. 150 to 250 inches of snow depending on the county. Plowing a long driveway, salt eating the vehicles, 4x4 with real snow tires. The Model Y loses 20 to 30% range and chargers thin out fast. Growing season is short.
  3. Car insurance. Michigan is no-fault, averages ~$3,073/yr for full coverage statewide. Rural UP rates run well under that and Michigan bans credit scoring, but budget $500 to $1,000/yr more per vehicle than NC. Zero property tax eats that on day one.
  4. Isolation and logistics. No Costco anywhere in the UP (Green Bay, Traverse City, Duluth). Marquette has Meijer, Walmart, Menards. Amazon shows up a day late. Contractors are thin, which doesn't matter if you DIY.
  5. Liquor is a control state. The MLCC sets minimum spirit prices statewide, so no banger liquor stores. Beer and wine are open market. Fix: fill the van every time we visit Laurel.
  6. Land due diligence. Wetland rules, riparian rights, township-level zoning, seasonal roads, parcels with no year-round access, and the Commercial Forest Program (a separate tax program with public-access strings). Cheap listings are cheap for a reason.
  7. Wildlife vs. dog. Wolves and porcupines are a real dog problem in the western UP. Ticks everywhere.

Fiber internet: yes, and improving

Highline is the one to watch. Its 2025 build ran 1,400 miles of fiber to 14,000 homes across Marquette, Iron, Schoolcraft, Luce, Mackinac and Chippewa counties at up to 2 Gbps. Summer 2026 extended into Marquette, Luce, Schoolcraft and Mackinac. Delta County is its strongest UP footprint. Michigan got $1.6B in BEAD money and Highline is chasing it for the UP. Target is 50,000 UP customers by 2027.

Fiber follows county roads and towns. A 40 at the end of a two-track will be Starlink. Check each address at Highline's lookup before you fall in love.

Shooting and chickens

Shooting. No statewide minimum distance for target shooting on your own land. The 450-ft (150 yd) safety zone around occupied buildings is a hunting rule (MCL 324.40111). Careless discharge law (MCL 752.863a) always applies. Townships can and do regulate discharge, so read the township ordinance before buying. 40 wooded acres with a berm is a non-issue almost everywhere in the UP.

Chickens. Township zoning controls. Ag or rural-residential zoning is fine; the Right to Farm Act protects commercial-scale operations that follow GAAMPs. With 40 acres the only requirement is neighbors who can put up with you, and at 40 acres there aren't any.

Where in the UP

Two orbits worth living in. Skip the far west (Ontonagon, Gogebic) unless the goal is cheap and alone.

Marquette orbit

Gwinn, Skandia, Ishpeming, Negaunee, Champion, Republic, Big Bay. The only real hospital and airport in the UP, NMU, the most listings, Highline actively building in Marquette County. Land is the priciest in the UP: figure $2,000 to $4,000/acre for a decent 40 with road frontage. Best pick if Mom comes.

150 to 200 inches of snow. 4.5 hours to Saukville.

Escanaba / Delta County orbit

Rapid River, Gladstone, Cornell, Garden Peninsula. OSF St. Francis hospital, Highline's strongest UP fiber footprint, Green Bay 90 minutes for anything Marquette doesn't have. Land is cheaper and the terrain is flatter and more farmable. Delta County median sale price is down 12% YoY, so there's negotiating room.

Less snow than Marquette (lake-effect shadow). 3.5 hours to Saukville.

Next steps

Before we buy anything

  • Pull the actual rating letter and confirm it says permanent and total. Michigan, Minnesota's $300k tier and Nebraska all require P&T, not just 100%.
  • Sequence the move after the CQA protected-work letters and Bekah's TDIU decision land, not during.
  • Run every finalist through Highline's address lookup.
  • Ask the county for the parcel count and the township for the discharge and zoning ordinances on each finalist.
  • Decide law school: in person means an apartment in Lansing or Ann Arbor for three years, remote means nothing changes.

After closing

  • Combine parcels at the county if there's more than one.
  • File Form 5107 with the township assessor before Dec 31 of the first tax year. Bring the P&T letter and DD-214.
  • DV plates at the SOS for both of us. Move the Accord's NC plate over.
  • Free DNR licenses with the VA letter. Carry a copy while hunting.
  • Keep the house parcel free of any business use. The van lives there, the business doesn't.
