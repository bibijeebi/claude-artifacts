Bottom Line for You
Baker is a fellowship-trained reproductive microsurgeon who spent 24 years as an Army urologist before joining Duke. She did her male infertility and microsurgery fellowship at the Cleveland Clinic (2011-2013), one of the top programs in the country. She's published outcomes on 526 vasectomy reversals with an 88.7% overall patency rate, which is excellent, especially given that her cohort included reversals out to 34 years post-vasectomy. Your 2-year interval puts you in her absolute best-outcome category. She's in the VA Community Care network, which means this is $0 to you. Duke Fertility Center in Morrisville, about 15 minutes from the house.

Key stat: In her published 14-year, 526-patient study at Madigan Army Medical Center, Baker's team achieved 88.7% patency regardless of reconstruction type, with no difference in outcomes between fellowship-trained and non-fellowship-trained surgeons when case volume was high. She was study designer and senior author.

Why She's a Strong Fit for You
Military veteran treating a military veteran. She spent 24 years in the same system you came from, treating soldiers, Marines, and their families. She did vasectomy reversals at Madigan on active duty servicemembers, which means she's seen your exact scenario (young military guy who got snipped, now wants kids) hundreds of times. She also worked at the Durham VA, so she understands Community Care referrals from the provider side.

Published research on exactly your procedure. Her 14-year, 526-patient study is one of the larger single-institution vasectomy reversal series in the literature. It demonstrated that high-volume surgeons (which she is) achieve excellent outcomes regardless of whether they have a fertility fellowship, and that resident participation doesn't hurt outcomes. The 88.7% patency rate across all reconstruction types (VV and VE combined) is on par with top published series.

Integrated fertility center. She operates within Duke Fertility Center, which has a full embryology lab, reproductive endocrinologists, and the whole IVF/IUI pipeline on site. If anything changes mid-procedure or you need follow-up fertility support, it's all under one roof.
