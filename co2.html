02 / Escalation

Six Tiers Deep

Each tier compounds on the one before it. You're standing on Tier 1. Tier 6 is a fully operational beverage lab. The path between is shorter than you think.

01
Current State

Small-Batch Carbonation

Sparkling water. Ranch water. Carbonated grapes. A soda fountain's worth of possibilities in a 2L bottle.

Everything the carbonator cap can reach. No other equipment needed. This is the baseline and it's already impressive.

  • Plain sparkling — Topo Chico clone with lime + pinch of salt
  • Pre-mix cocktails — Ranch water, G&T, vodka soda, Paloma, highballs
  • Fruit carbonation — Grapes, strawberries, orange slices. Pop Rocks in your mouth
  • Homemade soda — Ginger ale, cola (LorAnn cola flavor + syrup), tonic water
  • Party novelty — Carbonated pickles, watermelon, pineapple
Yield at this tier

2–3 L per session. Limited by bottle count and fridge space. Cost: pennies per liter.

02
First Upgrade
+$80

Keg Commercial Beer

Buy a keg of craft beer from a distributor. Transfer. Tap. Pour. Never buy six-packs again.

The fastest possible escalation. One used Cornelius keg off Facebook Marketplace, one liquid-out QD, one picnic tap. You're pouring draft beer from a fridge before the weekend ends.

Cost math: A 1/6 bbl keg of Bell's Two Hearted runs $85 at a local distributor. That's 660 oz. Equivalent in 6-packs costs $145+. You break even on the keg setup in 2 kegs.

Shopping list
  • Used 5-gal corny keg (FB Marketplace / Craigslist) — $40-60
  • Black liquid ball lock QD + barb — $10
  • Picnic tap with 5 ft liquid line — $15
  • Keg lube + o-ring kit — $8
  • Already have: gas QD, regulator, CO2 tank
03
The Rabbit Hole
+$150

Homebrew Beer

Brew 5 gallons of your own pale ale. Transfer to the corny. Force-carb at 12 PSI for 48 hours. Drink pints you made from grain.

The homesteader gateway. You're already brewing kombucha-adjacent thinking: steep, boil, ferment, transfer, carbonate. Starter kit + kettle + yeast = actual beer in ~3 weeks.

  • Easy first brews — American pale ale, cream ale, hefeweizen, brown ale
  • Skip for now — Lagers (temp control), big IPAs (hop burn), sours (contamination risk)
  • Grain-to-glass cost — ~$40 for 5 gallons = 40 pints at $1/pint
Entry path
  • 1-gal Northern Brewer kit to learn the process — $45
  • Scale to 5-gal: kettle, fermenter, airlock, siphon — $100
  • Transfer to corny keg you already bought in Tier 2
  • Force-carb with your existing rig at 12 PSI × 48 hrs
04
Draft System
+$400

Keezer & Nitro

A chest freezer with 2-4 taps through the collar. Temperature controller holds it at 38°F. This is the end-state and it's genuinely achievable in a weekend.

Find a used 7 cu ft chest freezer on Marketplace for $100. Inkbird temp controller ($35). Wood collar ($40 lumber). Shanks + faucets + tap handles ($120 for 2 taps). Done.

  • Two taps — Homebrew on one, commercial keg on the other
  • Three taps — Add a permanent sparkling water line
  • Nitro coffee — 75/25 N2/CO2 blend through a stout faucet. Restaurant charges $7, costs you $0.30
  • Nitro stout — Same setup for Guinness-style beer
Pro move

Keep one tap dedicated to plain carbonated water for Bekah. Eliminates La Croix entirely. Ongoing cost: $25 refills every 6–9 months.

06
Off-Label

Non-Beverage Uses

The CO2 tank is a utility, not just a drink machine. Weird applications most people never consider.
  • Planted aquarium injection — Same regulator, add bubble counter + diffuser. Turbocharges plant growth in freshwater tanks
  • Paintball tank refills — With CGA-320 to CGA-320 adapter, fill paintball tanks at home
  • Fermentation O2 purge — Blanket carboy headspace with CO2 to prevent oxidation during racking
  • Dry ice snow (emergency) — Vent through a pillowcase, collect CO2 snow. Impractical but works
  • Pest control — CO2 euthanasia for rodent traps. Humane, relevant to rabbit processing
  • Tire inflation (emergency) — Not ideal but works for seating tubeless bike tires in a pinch
