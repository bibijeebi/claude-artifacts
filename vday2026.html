Good Luck, Have Fun, Don't Die
R · 2h 15m · Dir: Gore Verbinski · Sam Rockwell, Haley Lu Richardson, Zazie Beetz, Michael Peña
87% Audience 84% Critics 7.6 IMDb
A time traveler walks into an LA diner at 10:10 PM, announces he's there to save the world, and needs volunteers. This is his 117th attempt. A rogue AI is about to end humanity and only the right combination of random diner patrons can stop it. Sam Rockwell absolutely rips.
GenreSci-fi action comedy / time loop VibeIrreverent, inventive, funny with real stakes. Not a dumb comedy. Compared ToEverything Everywhere All at Once meets Groundhog Day. Verbinski back in peak form. V-Day FactorNot romantic at all, but it's the best-reviewed movie playing. Fun shared experience. Heads UpNot at Alamo or Fenton. Regal theaters only. Third act gets a little messy but audiences leave buzzing.
Most original studio movie in theaters right now. If you want the best pure film, this is it. Gore Verbinski (Pirates 1, Rango) firing on all cylinders. People are saying they want to see it twice.

Crime 101
R · 1h 53m · Dir: Bart Layton · Chris Hemsworth, Mark Ruffalo, Halle Berry, Barry Keoghan
85% Critics 84% Audience
Hemsworth plays a jewel thief pulling audacious heists along the 101 freeway. Ruffalo is the detective chasing him. Barry Keoghan and Halle Berry round out the crew. Based on a Don Winslow novella. Slick, fun, classic cat-and-mouse crime thriller.
GenreHeist / crime thriller VibeStylish throwback. Cool soundtrack, sharp editing, great cast chemistry. Compared ToHeat, The Town, Ocean's Eleven. Classic heist energy. V-Day FactorSolid "fun movie" pick. Hemsworth is charming, Berry is great. Easy crowd-pleaser. Best FeatureShortest runtime (1h53m). Playing at both Alamo and Fenton.
If you liked Heat, The Italian Job, or any slick heist movie, you'll enjoy this. Hemsworth is apparently outstanding. The shortest option if you don't want to commit to 2+ hours.

Wuthering Heights
R · 2h 16m · Dir: Emerald Fennell · Margot Robbie, Jacob Elordi, Hong Chau
84% Audience 64% Critics
Emerald Fennell (Saltburn, Promising Young Woman) reimagines the Brontë gothic romance. Margot Robbie and Jacob Elordi in a steamy, intense, wildly stylish love story on the moors. Charli XCX on the soundtrack. Very much an R-rated romance.
GenreGothic romance / drama VibeSteamy, dark, stylish. More Saltburn than Jane Austen. Heavy on the visuals. Compared ToSaltburn's energy with a classic love story. Style over substance for some. V-Day Factor🌹 THE Valentine's Day pick. This is the one Alamo is building their whole V-Day programming around. Critics Split64% critics vs 84% audience. Critics say style over substance. Audiences vibing hard.
If Bekah liked Saltburn, she'll want this. The most "Valentine's Day" movie by far. Alamo is running it in like 16 showtimes with "XOXO, ALAMO" branding and themed cocktails. Not for everyone, but the audience score speaks.
