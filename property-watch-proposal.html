Why this setup

Why it's the right call

One app for everything you watch. The doorbell and the front camera both sit in your Apple Home app, next to your other Apple things. No new account, no second app to learn.
No monthly fees, anywhere. A Ring-style system charges per home, forever, just to see your own video. Every piece here records for free.
The driveway alarm ignores wildlife. Only a vehicle's metal trips the buried sensor; the deer and turkeys don't. It dings for cars, nothing else.
Covers every approach. The doorbell takes the front door, the camera takes the driveway and yard, and the sensor catches vehicles before they reach the house. Three angles, one system, one app.
Two honest notes, stated plainly:
  • The front camera's install depends on which option above you pick, anywhere from a plug-in WiFi camera on the house with almost no work, to a buried PoE run out to the flagpole, which is a real trench best cut with a rented trencher. The cost line reflects that spread.
  • The driveway sensor's probe gets buried about a foot deep beside the drive, a small one-time dig, not under it, so the plow never touches it. Its outdoor battery gets swapped about once a year, and lithium AAs hold up best through Wisconsin winters.
