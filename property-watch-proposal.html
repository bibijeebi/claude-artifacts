Why this setup

Why it's the right call

One app for all of it. The doorbell, the optional camera, and the garage monitor all sit in your Apple Home app, next to your other Apple things. No new account, no second app to learn.
No monthly fees, anywhere. A Ring-style system charges per home, forever, just to see your own video. Every piece here records for free.
The driveway alarm ignores wildlife. Only a vehicle's metal trips the buried sensor; the deer and turkeys don't. It dings for cars, nothing else.
Each piece earns its spot. The doorbell owns faces at the door, the driveway sensor catches vehicles, and the optional camera and garage monitor each answer a different question. No two doing the same job, so you only buy what you'll use.
Two honest notes, stated plainly:
  • If you add the front camera, its install is the one variable: anywhere from a plug-in WiFi camera on the house with almost no work, to a buried PoE run out to the flagpole, which is a real trench best cut with a rented trencher. The core two pieces go in quick either way.
  • The driveway sensor's probe gets buried about a foot deep beside the drive, a small one-time dig, not under it, so the plow never touches it. Its outdoor battery gets swapped about once a year, and lithium AAs hold up best through Wisconsin winters.
