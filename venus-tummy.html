primer

So what's a "venus tummy"?

A venus tummy is the soft little pouch of lower-belly fat that sits just below the navel and gets more visible in tight skirts or dresses. The shape isn't new. The name is. It got attached to the look around September 2025, but the underlying meme, marble statues of Venus and Aphrodite sculpted with a rounded belly and stomach rolls, had been circulating for years before anyone slapped a label on it.

The label comes from the goddess and from anatomy: the mons veneris, literally "mound of Venus," the soft fleshy rise low on the abdomen. Body-positivity TikTok grabbed it fast and ran, reframing a feature people used to suck in as a goddess trait worth flexing. The "I Believe In X Supremacy" meme format did the rest.

"A friend just told me this is a venus tummy. A goddess belly. So mine was never supposed to be flat." // paraphrasing the body-positivity tiktok take

Of course, X being X, the term landed in the middle of a fight, half the internet calling it the most attractive thing a woman can have, the other half telling women to "remove the ball." That argument is the soil the police-sketch joke grew in.

glossary for the lost

Four things you now have to know

venus tummy

noun · slang · est. sep 2025

The soft lower-belly pouch below the navel, named after the goddess Venus and the marble statues sculpted with one. Reclaimed by body-positivity TikTok from an insecurity into a "goddess belly." Also a recurring X flashpoint over whether it's attractive.

mons veneris

latin · "mound of venus"

The fleshy rise low on the abdomen, anatomy's actual term, the goddess's name baked right in. This is the bridge that lets @gurgle_io's brain skid from "venus tummy" straight to "pubic." The pun was always sitting there.

Nair "short shorts"

ad jingle · 1970s–80s

"Who wears short shorts?" The hair-removal brand built decades of brand equity on a four-note jingle lifted from the 1958 doo-wop hit "Short Shorts" by The Royal Teens. A Gen-X earworm summoned here as a "clarification" that clarifies nothing.

Gem / Tung Tung Sahur

italian brainrot · feb 2025

Tung Tung Tung Sahur is an anthropomorphic wooden drum with a bat, an Indonesian Ramadan wake-up tradition turned AI meme monster (lore: ignore the sahur call three times and he visits). "Gem Sahur" is the bedazzled, crystal-coated remix. Posted as pure derailment.
