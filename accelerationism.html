Case Study: SF Reparations

How accelerationists read progressive policy

December 2024: San Francisco creates "Reparations Fund" framework.
Proposal includes $5 million per eligible Black resident.
~46,000 Black residents in SF. City has $1B deficit.

No money actually allocated. Just a framework for future donations.

The Accelerationist Read

LEFT Accelerationist

"Push it further. Make the contradictions visible. When the system can't deliver $230 billion in reparations, people will see capitalism can't solve racial inequality."

RIGHT Accelerationist

"Perfect example of Cathedral dysfunction. Progressive cities making impossible promises while actual problems fester. This accelerates collapse of democratic legitimacy."

Both want the same thing: systemic breakdown. Just different endgames.

The Yarvin Analysis

"Very unpopular policies like mass immigration persist despite strong majorities being against them. So the question of 'Is democracy good or bad?' is secondary to 'Is it what we actually have?'"

In Yarvin's framework: SF reparations = Cathedral virtue signaling.
Elites make symbolic gestures while ignoring material conditions.
This "proves" democracy doesn't work and justifies authoritarian alternatives.

What's Actually Happening

• SF formally apologized for urban renewal displacement (1940s-70s Fillmore district)

• Advisory committee spent 3 years producing 100+ recommendations

• Fund created with zero city money — relies on private donations

• Mayor Lurie: "We are not allocating money to this fund"

• Black population: 5% of SF, but 37% of homeless population

Real policy or symbolic gesture? Both sides have ammunition.
