Executive Summary

Eleven options within 30 minutes of the Elgin residence at 374 Turnwall Lane. Hotel suites, monthly Airbnb stays, Furnished Finder rentals, RV park living, on-post military lodging, a 55+ active adult community, and corporate housing all reviewed against the same criteria.

Priority weighting: (1) proximity to the marital residence in Elgin, (2) preservation of capital pending equitable distribution, (3) flexibility — no long-term lock-in given uncertain timeline, (4) suitability for a 75-year-old with prescribed oxygen.

Designed for traveling professionals on 3 to 12 month stays. Real apartments and houses, all-inclusive monthly pricing, no broker fees, no hotel feel. Best fit if displacement runs past six months. Listings turn over weekly so book promptly.

Typical monthly: $1,400 to $2,500 for a furnished 1 to 2 BR in 29223 / 29045

Key trade-offs

Cost spread: RV park life is cheapest on paper (~$655/mo + RV) but adds capital outlay and lifestyle compromise. Hotel suites range $1,200 to $4,500. Wildewood Downs requires home purchase plus a monthly service fee.

Pet weight ceilings: Every hotel option caps pet weight at 75 to 80 lbs. A Saint Bernard at 120 to 180 lbs will not qualify at any chain hotel. Furnished Finder, Airbnb, traditional rentals, or RV are the only options that can host a large dog during visits.

Glenforest School: All Northeast / Elgin options are 23 to 27 miles from 1041 Harbor Dr, West Columbia. Drive time roughly 30 to 40 minutes. The school commute does not meaningfully differentiate the options.

Veteran discounts: IHG Army Hotels, Marriott (Gov't & Military rate), Hilton (Honors military rate), and WoodSpring (Operation Homefront program) all honor retiree status. Quote the discount at booking.

Oxygen + Wi-Fi: Every option below has reliable free Wi-Fi and standard 110V outlets adequate for a home concentrator. The RV options need a 50-amp full-hookup site (confirmed available at all three RV parks listed).
