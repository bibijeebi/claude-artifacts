Stocked means NCWRC raised fish in a hatchery and released them into the waterbody. This maintains fishable populations where natural reproduction can't keep up, like mountain trout streams or urban ponds. Trout streams get restocked weekly during season. Catfish ponds might get one annual drop.

Wild means the fish reproduce naturally. These populations are self-sustaining. Wild fish are generally warier and harder to catch than recently stocked fish, but often better eating.

Practical takeaway: Want guaranteed action? Hit a recently stocked site. Want a challenge? Target wild populations. NC's trout are ~90% stocked. Largemouth bass are 99% wild.
