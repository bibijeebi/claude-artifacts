Adam Green

Know More News
Active since ~2014United StatesConspiracy Theorist

Adam Green is an antisemitic conspiracy theorist and the creator of Know More News, a media operation that has spanned YouTube (terminated), a website, Patreon, and various alternative platforms. He self-describes as an "independent journalist, documentary filmmaker, geopolitical analyst."

Content & Ideology

Green's output is overwhelmingly focused on a single thesis: that Zionist Jews secretly control the world, orchestrated 9/11, control the US government and media, and are working toward global domination through Talmudic and Kabbalistic religious prophecy. His catalog includes titles like "The Zionist Plan to Take Over the World" (120K+ views), "Jewish Kabbalah Predicts the Destruction of the Gentiles," and "Noahide Laws: The One World Religion." He has described COVID-19 as potentially an Israeli operation and called Bitcoin a "Zionist scam."

Significance in the Meme
The original meme video was posted from Green's Know More News account, making him both a subject of the meme and its original creator/distributor. This is the video whose faces we identified.

Green's relationship to Alex Jones is adversarial: he considers Jones and right-wing media to be "controlled opposition" for Israel. Infowars host Owen Shroyer appeared on Green's channel in 2018 and conceded that "Jewish conspiracies" were real while arguing Jones simply focused on other issues.

Deplatforming

Green's primary YouTube channel was terminated for Terms of Service violations. A secondary channel was also terminated. He continues operating through his website (knowmorenews.org) and alternative platforms. The ADL, SPLC, and Canary Mission all track Green as a significant antisemitic content creator.
