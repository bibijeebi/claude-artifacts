Eric Orwoll — Return to the Land
b. late 1980s-early 1990sNorthern ArkansasWhite Community Builder

Eric Orwoll, who uses the Norwegian version of his surname "Aarvoll" online, is the founder of Return to the Land (RTTL), a white nationalist "intentional community" movement in the Arkansas Ozarks, and a rising figure in white identitarian media through his close collaboration with Keith Woods.

Return to the Land

RTTL, operating under the corporate name Wisdom Woods LLC, was co-founded by Orwoll and Peter Csere in 2023. The organization owns over 160 acres near Ravenden, Arkansas, operating as a "private membership association" exclusively for white people of European descent. Jewish people are explicitly barred (not considered white by RTTL). As of mid-2025, the first community housed approximately 40 inhabitants, with a second Arkansas site opened and expansion planned into Appalachia and Tennessee. The community features cabins, roads, wells, a community center, and a schoolhouse.

Orwoll claims the PMA structure exempts RTTL from the Civil Rights Act and Fair Housing Act, though legal experts dispute this.

Background

Orwoll says he was formerly a musician for Shen Yun, the Falun Gong-affiliated performing arts organization promoted by the Epoch Times. He presents as a "Christian Platonist" intellectual, hosting the Aarvoll YouTube channel and co-hosting the Understanding Platonism podcast with Keith Woods.

On Hitler and violence: Orwoll characterized public opinion on Adolf Hitler as "one-sided" and predicted a "second coming": "You're going to get Hitler 2.0 when you deserve him." He endorsed Patriot Front and confirmed he was "indirectly referring to Patriot Front" when posting about groups that "know how to organize and deploy men." Co-founder Csere posted "1488" (the Fourteen Words / "Heil Hitler") in the RTTL Telegram chat. When challenged about telling someone "you might not know enough about Jews yet," Orwoll called it "tongue in cheek."
Network & Vision

Orwoll has appeared on nearly two dozen far-right media programs including the Stew Peters Show, was announced as a guest at Fuentes's AFPAC VI, and voted Trump 2024 (only because he considered Harris worse). He draws inspiration from Peter Thiel-backed "Network State" concepts, envisioning "a network of a dozen high tech, high human capital, small White cities spread throughout the US south."
