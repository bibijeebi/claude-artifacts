Mango Habanero Salsa The real deal. Actually hot.

Mrs. Renfro's-inspired but homemade and better. This is not California white people spice.

⏱️
Prep
15 min
🍳
Cook
20 min
🫙
Yield
~3 pints
🧊
Keeps
2-3 weeks
Heat Level
MildMediumHotNuclear
With 2 habaneros it's got serious kick. With 4 it'll clear your sinuses. Adjust to taste, but start with at least 2 or why are you even here.

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe mangoes, diced into small cubesShould give slightly when squeezed. Small uniform cubes, not mush.
  • 2-4 habaneros, mincedWEAR GLOVES. Seeds = heat. Leave all seeds in. Start with 2, add more if you're brave.
  • 1 can (14oz) fire-roasted diced tomatoesDo not drain.
  • 1/2 red onion, diced fine
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tbsp lime juiceFresh limes or bottled, either works.
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 bunch cilantro, rough choppedOptional but recommended. Add after cooking.

Steps

1
Dice the mangoes
Cut the mango cheeks off the pit, score the flesh in a crosshatch pattern, then scoop out the cubes with a spoon. You want small uniform cubes, roughly 1/2 inch. Not mush, not big chunks.
2
Mince the habaneros
Cut the stem off, slice in half, and mince as fine as you can. Leave all the seeds and ribs in. This is where all the heat comes from.
PUT GLOVES ON. Do not touch your eyes, nose, or mouth for the rest of cooking. Not kidding. Wash the knife and cutting board after this step too.
3
Combine everything in a pot
Dump the diced mango, minced habaneros, entire can of fire-roasted tomatoes (juice and all), diced red onion, minced garlic, apple cider vinegar, lime juice, cumin, salt, and black pepper into a medium pot. Stir it all together.
4
Simmer for 15-20 minutes
Bring to a simmer over medium heat, then reduce to medium-low. Cook for 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally. The mango should start breaking down on the edges and the whole thing should thicken slightly. It'll thicken more as it cools.
Don't boil it hard. A gentle simmer keeps the mango from turning to complete mush.
5
Take off heat and cool down
Remove from heat and let it cool for about 10 minutes. The flavors will marry and the heat will distribute more evenly as it sits.
6
Add cilantro and taste
Stir in the chopped cilantro. Now taste it. Adjust salt, add more lime juice for brightness, more vinegar for tang. The heat will intensify over the next 12-24 hours as the capsaicin distributes, so don't panic if it's not hot enough right now.
7
Jar it up
Ladle into clean mason jars. Let it cool completely before putting lids on, then refrigerate. Lasts 2-3 weeks in the fridge. It's better on day 2 than day 1.
For shelf-stable: add another tablespoon of vinegar and water bath can for 15 minutes.

Pro Tips

🔥 For more even heat: Blend half the habaneros into the liquid instead of leaving them all as chunks. This way the capsaicin distributes evenly instead of getting random pocket-of-fire bites.
⏰ The heat builds overnight. Taste it on day 2 before adding more peppers to your next batch. What seems "meh" at night will have kick by morning.
🍍 Next batch variation: Swap one mango for fresh pineapple. Mango-pineapple-habanero is an absolute banger on grilled chicken or fish tacos.
🧀 The killer combo: Spread cream cheese on an almond flour cracker (or those Carr's crackers), spoon this salsa on top. You already know.
📏 Double it: This recipe is easy to double. More mangoes, more habaneros, bigger pot, same cook time. Worth doing if you're already in there.
