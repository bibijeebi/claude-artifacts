CQA Target Neighborhoods: Homes Built 2006–2011

Wake County assessor parcel data · pulled Aug 20, 2026 · 4-mile radius around each crew address
BLUF: 52 subdivisions within ~4 miles of the three addresses where the majority of homes are 15–20 years old. Best density is around Marcus and Devin in SE Raleigh — Battle Ridge North alone is ~300 qualifying homes. Around Benny, the action is the Hwy 42 / Fuquay-Varina corridor: Sterling Ridge, Lakestone Village, Bloomfield. Every neighborhood name links to Google Maps.

Why this age band sells: these houses are on original builder-grade flex duct and panned returns that have never been cleaned, the original HVAC equipment is at or past end-of-life (so coils and new-system protection are live topics), and many are on their second or third owner who never had the ducts touched. Mid-2000s construction also means construction debris sealed in the runs from day one.

How to read it: Homes = residential parcels counted in the subdivision's 2006–2011-heavy phases (min 25). % = share of those built 2006–2011. Source: Wake County GIS tax parcels (YEAR_BUILT, single-family + townhome use codes). Phases of the same subdivision that fell below 50% in-window aren't counted, so a few totals understate full neighborhood size.
