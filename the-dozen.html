The Gauntlet Nobody Ran

The Surname Test

Every first name is half a name. Foreman is a hard surname to pair: a trochee ending in -man, starting with F, carrying the stressed syllable FORE, and it's a literal job title with a heavyweight champion attached. Six rules, applied.

1 · Rhythm

Mismatched syllable counts flow best. FORE-man loves one-syllable punches and three-syllable runs.

2 · The Junction

The last sound of the first name meets the F. Vowels, stops, and nasals hand off clean; fricatives smear.

3 · No Internal Rhyme

Nothing that echoes FORE. Any name ending in an -or / -ora sound sing-songs straight into the surname.

4 · No Accidental Alliteration

F-names read comic-book against Foreman. Fine if deliberate. It wasn't.

5 · Semantic Collision

Foreman is a real word, so the full name gets parsed as a phrase. Title words and adjectives are the danger class.
