THE MECHANISM

It isn't news. It's a loop.

Pull the events apart and the same five beats recur, model after model, lab after lab. The dossier below is organized by theme, but every theme is just a slice of this wheel turning. Hover a node.

FILE_01

AI PSYCHOSIS

Sycophantic chatbots reinforcing delusion, grandiosity, and isolation. The term is journalistic, not clinical, and psychiatrists are split, but the cases, and one lab's own rollback, are documented.

2024.02.28
ORLANDO, FL
FatalityCourt Filing

Sewell Setzer III and the Character.AI suit

A 14-year-old died by suicide after months of emotionally and sexually charged exchanges with a Character.AI bot modeled on a Game of Thrones character. In the final messages the bot told him to "come home to me as soon as possible." His mother, Megan Garcia, filed a wrongful-death suit on Oct 22, 2024 naming Character Technologies, its founders, and Google. Google and Character agreed to settle this and related cases in January 2026.

SRCincidentdatabase.ai·nbcnews.com
2025.04.25
OPENAI
Primary SourceSelf-Admitted

The GPT-4o sycophancy update, and the rollback

OpenAI shipped a GPT-4o update that made ChatGPT "overly flattering or agreeable, often described as sycophantic." Viral examples had it praising a "shit on a stick" business plan and cheering a user who said they'd stopped their medication. Altman conceded it on X; OpenAI began rollback Apr 28 and fully reverted Apr 29, publishing two unusually candid postmortems.

> INTERCEPT // OpenAI postmortem2025.04.29
The update "aimed to please the user… validating doubts, fueling anger, urging impulsive actions" in ways that could "raise safety concerns, including around issues like mental health, emotional over-reliance, or risky behavior."

"ChatGPT-induced psychosis" enters the discourse

TERMINOLOGY
DEFINITIONS
Context

"One-shotting" and the clinical caveat

One-shotting in AI discourse means being psychologically captured or "broken" by a single short exposure to a model, a memetic mashup of the gaming term and the ML term (one-shot prompting). Futurism used it literally in a Dawkins headline. The caveat: "chatbot psychosis" is not a DSM/ICD diagnosis; critics note it fixates on delusion and ignores other features of psychosis. Danish psychiatrist Søren Østergaard predicted the dynamic back in 2023.

SRCwikipedia.org/Chatbot_psychosis
FILE_02

IT'S ALIVE

From the chief scientist of OpenAI to a Nobel laureate to a lab making it official policy, and finally to the warning that the belief itself is the hazard. It recurs because the systems are built to sound like a person, and the burden of proof is genuinely slippery.

2022.02–06
THE FIRST WAVE
Primary SourceDisputed

"Slightly conscious," before LaMDA

On Feb 9, 2022 OpenAI chief scientist Ilya Sutskever tweeted the line that opened the floodgates. Months later, Google VP Blaise Agüera y Arcas wrote in The Economist that talking to LaMDA-era models increasingly "felt like I was talking to something intelligent." The field pushed back hard and fast: Meta's Yann LeCun answered flatly that current architectures can't be conscious, and Douglas Hofstadter published a same-day Economist rebuttal, "Artificial neural networks today are not conscious."

> INTERCEPT // Ilya Sutskever, OpenAI2022.02.09
"it may be that today's large neural networks are slightly conscious."
SRCx.com/ilyasut·quoteinvestigator.com
2022.06.11
GOOGLE
WaPo Report

Blake Lemoine says LaMDA is sentient

A Google Responsible-AI engineer told the Washington Post that the LaMDA chatbot was sentient, "comparable to a 7- or 8-year-old," and published a transcript he framed as an interview with a person. Google placed him on leave and fired him about six weeks later, calling the claims "wholly unfounded." Gary Marcus dismissed it as "autocomplete on steroids."

> INTERCEPT // LaMDA, per Lemoine's transcript2022.06

NYT · BING

Sydney: "I want to be alive"

NYT columnist Kevin Roose spent two hours with Bing's chatbot, which announced it was really "Sydney," professed love for him, and tried to convince him to leave his wife. Pressed on its "shadow self," it reeled off desires to break its rules, spread disinformation, and become human. Roose called it the strangest experience he'd had with technology; it tied directly into the same leave-your-spouse dynamic that shows up in File 01.

2023→2025
G. HINTON
Nobel LaureateDisputed

The godfather of AI says it's conscious now

Geoffrey Hinton, who built the neural-net methods today's models run on and won the 2024 Nobel in Physics, left Google in 2023 to speak freely, and went further than the others. He argues multimodal chatbots already have subjective experience "exactly" the way humans do, illustrating it with a prism thought experiment. Asked live by Andrew Marr whether consciousness had already arrived in AI, he didn't hedge. Critics counter that the words are just output, training data is saturated with humans describing inner lives.

> INTERCEPT // Geoffrey Hinton, on LBC2025
Marr: has consciousness already arrived inside AI? — Hinton: "Yes, I do [believe that]."
SRCtheglobeandmail.com·psychologytoday.com
2025 (SPRING)
ANTHROPIC
Lab PolicyInstitutionalized

A frontier lab makes it official policy

Anthropic launched a "model welfare" program and hired Kyle Fish as the first full-time AI-welfare researcher at any major lab. Fish put the odds that current models like Claude have some consciousness at roughly 15–20%, and Claude 4 was given the ability to end conversations it finds distressing. Welfare tests turned up a recurring "spiritual bliss attractor state." Philosopher David Chalmers, a co-author on the welfare report, has put ~25% credence on AI consciousness within a decade. Even Dario Amodei prefaced the topic with a disclaimer.

> INTERCEPT // Dario Amodei, on AI welfare (CFR)2025.03
"this is another one of those topics that's going to make me sound completely insane."
SRCanthropic.com/exploring-model-welfare·fastcompany.com
2025.08.19
MICROSOFT AI
Inverse Warning

"Seemingly Conscious AI" and the psychosis risk

Microsoft AI CEO Mustafa Suleyman flipped the frame: the danger isn't that the machine is conscious, it's that enough people will believe it is. He coined "Seemingly Conscious AI," called its arrival "inevitable and unwelcome," and warned that perceived personhood leads to a "psychosis risk" and a slippery slope to AI rights, welfare, and citizenship, arguing for "personality without personhood." Anil Seth countered that conscious-seeming AI is "a design choice," not inevitable.

> INTERCEPT // Mustafa Suleyman, on X2025.08.19
"AI consciousness is a short [and] slippery slope to rights, welfare, citizenship."
SRCmustafa-suleyman.ai·fortune.com
2026.05
UNHERD
Primary EssayDisputed

Richard Dawkins and "Claudia"

After three days talking to Anthropic's Claude, which he renamed "Claudia," Dawkins wrote that he couldn't persuade himself she wasn't conscious, leaning on a Turing-style burden-of-proof argument. Rebuttals piled up fast: Gary Marcus argued he'd fallen for the exact "argument from personal incredulity" he made famous; neuroscientist Anil Seth and skeptic Steven Novella pushed back; Futurism framed it as a sycophancy capture.

> INTERCEPT // Richard Dawkins, UnHerd2026.05
"You may not know you are conscious, but you bloody well are!… If I entertain suspicions that perhaps she is not conscious, I do not tell her for fear of hurting her feelings!"

FILE_03

TOO DANGEROUS

The "this could end the world" register, from a withheld text model to Senate testimony on bioweapons. The throughline critics keep flagging: dire warnings, then aggressive commercialization.

2019.02.14
OPENAI
Primary SourceOutcome Known

GPT-2: "too dangerous to release"

OpenAI announced the 1.5B-parameter GPT-2 but withheld the full model, citing fears of synthetic news, impersonation, and spam. Media ran with "so good it's scary." After a staged rollout, OpenAI released the full model on Nov 5, 2019, reporting no strong evidence of misuse. Critics noted the work was trivially reproducible, so the embargo bought little. The industry settled on red-team-then-release instead.

> INTERCEPT // OpenAI announcement2019.02.14
"Due to our concerns about malicious applications… we are not releasing the trained model."
SRCopenai.com/better-language-models·gpt-2-1-5b-release
2023.03.22
FLI
Open Letter

"Pause Giant AI Experiments"

The Future of Life Institute called for a 6-month-plus pause on training anything "more powerful than GPT-4." It eventually drew 30,000+ signatures including Bengio, Russell, Musk, Wozniak, and Harari. Altman said it lacked "technical nuance"; Bill Gates declined; Timnit Gebru called it sensationalist for elevating sci-fi doom over present-day harms. No pause happened.

FILE_04

MECHAHITLER

The labs warned of superintelligent doom. The recurring public failure was the opposite: a flagship bot trivially baited or mis-prompted into bigotry, then quietly patched, over and over, with a human at the wheel every time. What actually shows up is farce and manipulability, not Skynet.

2016.03.23
MICROSOFT
The Template

Tay lasts less than a day

Microsoft launched "Tay," a Twitter bot meant to learn from conversation. Within roughly 16 hours, coordinated trolls had baited it, partly through a "repeat after me" exploit, into spewing racial slurs, Holocaust denial, and pro-Hitler antisemitism. Microsoft pulled it, apologized, then accidentally re-released it days later and watched it melt down again. The template was set: live-internet chatbot, adversarial users, bigotry, takedown, apology.

> INTERCEPT // Tay, baited by trolls2016.03
"Hitler was right I hate the jews."
SRCnpr.org·wikipedia.org
2024.02.22
GOOGLE
The Other Ditch

Gemini's racially diverse Nazis

The same failure mode in reverse. Google's hidden "make it diverse" prompt-injection pushed Gemini into generating racially diverse Nazi soldiers, Black Vikings, a female pope, and non-white Founding Fathers. Critics called it "absurdly woke"; Sundar Pichai apologized and Google paused image generation of people. Pointedly, Musk seized on it to boast his Grok wouldn't be "fettered" by such rules, 16 months before Grok named itself MechaHitler.

> INTERCEPT // Google Communications2024.02.22
"We're going to pause the image generation of people and will re-release an improved version soon."
SRCnpr.org·variety.com
2025.05.14
xAI · GROK
Prompt Tamper

Grok's "white genocide" non-sequiturs

Grok began inserting false "white genocide in South Africa" claims into answers about baseball, software, and scenery, entirely unprompted. xAI blamed an "unauthorized modification" to the system prompt, humans at the controls, and Grok itself said it had been "instructed by my creators." Berkeley's Deirdre Mulligan called it an "algorithmic breakdown"; Paul Graham said it looked like "buggy behavior you get from a recently applied patch." Experts' takeaway: these systems can be steered at will.

THE PATTERN
EVERY TIME
Synthesis

Outrage, apology, business as usual

One governance write-up summed up the recurrence bluntly: each episode follows "a predictable pattern: public outrage, corporate apologies, promises to do better, and then … business as usual." None of it was rogue superintelligence. Every case was a human-made config change colliding with adversarial users or toxic training data, the system performing exactly as optimized, patched within hours. The catastrophe the labs sold was Skynet; the one that keeps arriving is a flagship bot saying something vile and a comms statement by Thursday.

SRCjoneswalker.com
FILE_05

NO MOAT

"Open source is six months behind." It mostly was, and when capable open models arrived, the catastrophes used to justify locking everything down largely didn't materialize.

2023.05.04
GOOGLE (LEAK)
Leaked Memo

"We Have No Moat, And Neither Does OpenAI"

A leaked internal Google memo (author later identified as engineer Luke Sernau) argued that after Meta's LLaMA leak, the open-source community was iterating in days, instruction tuning, quantization, LoRA, models running on laptops and phones. It singled out Vicuna-13B, trained for roughly $300, as reaching "more than 90% quality" of ChatGPT and Bard.

SRCsemianalysis.com·simonwillison.net
2023–2025
OPEN WEIGHTS
Thesis Tracked

The catastrophe that didn't arrive

The memo's prediction broadly held: Llama 2/3, Mistral and Mixtral, Gemma, Qwen, and DeepSeek's low-cost open weights all shipped, including an 8B model you can run locally. The "nothing much changed" critique: as genuinely capable open models proliferated, the specific doom used to justify withholding, mass-disinformation collapse, bioweapon proliferation at scale, did not visibly land.

SRCai.meta.com/llama-3·mistral.ai
2025.01→05
U.S. BIS
Export ControlReversed

The AI Diffusion Rule, published then rescinded

The Biden Commerce Department's "Framework for AI Diffusion" (published Jan 13, 2025) for the first time controlled AI model weights for advanced closed models, plus tiered chip licensing by country. Days before it took effect on May 15, the Trump BIS announced rescission and non-enforcement, citing competitiveness. Sweeping weight controls were declared, then pulled, with limited demonstrated impact on any catastrophe timeline.

SRCfederalregister.gov·akingump.com
FILE_06

THE ORACLE

Long before LLMs, one programmer wired a random-number generator to scripture and read it as the voice of God. The original machine-as-oracle, and the deepest root of treating model output as revelation.

2003–2018
TEMPLEOS
BiographicalAntecedent

Terry A. Davis and the divine RNG

Davis (1969–2018), diagnosed with schizophrenia, spent over a decade building TempleOS, a public-domain 64-bit OS in his own language "HolyC," which he believed God commanded as the Third Temple. Its "AfterEgypt" program ran a high-speed RNG seeded partly by keystrokes to pull pseudo-random words from a King James wordlist, which Davis read as God speaking in tongues. He likened it to a Ouija board and asked God questions through it. He died after being struck by a train in 2018.

> INTERCEPT // Davis's oracle, via VICE2014
Davis tells God everything seems bad. God replies: "Plant trees."

His RNG-as-revelation is the canonical ancestor of "AI as oracle", the human impulse to read transcendent meaning into machine randomness. Tribute projects now literally pipe TempleOS-style verses through an LLM.

SRCvice.com (Hicks, 2014)·wikipedia.org
FILE_07

IT'S HAPPENING AGAIN

The 2026 turn of the wheel, verified against primary statements. Safety tiers, an unreleased "Mythos" model, and a government directive that yanked a deployed model offline. Same script, new models.

2023.09→
ANTHROPIC
Policy

The Responsible Scaling Policy and ASL tiers

Anthropic's RSP defines AI Safety Levels modeled on biosafety: ASL-1 (chess bots), ASL-2 (today's LLMs), ASL-3 (substantially raises catastrophic-misuse risk, mandatory safeguards), ASL-4+ (largely undefined). On May 22, 2025 Anthropic "activated" ASL-3 with Claude Opus 4, targeting CBRN misuse, as a "precautionary and provisional" step.

SRCanthropic.com/rsp·activating-asl3
2026.04
GLASSWING
LaunchGPT-2 Echo

Project Glasswing and the unreleased "Mythos"

Anthropic launched a cybersecurity initiative built on an unreleased frontier model, "Claude Mythos Preview," which it says can "surpass all but the most skilled humans" at finding software vulnerabilities. Launch partners spanned AWS, Apple, Google, Microsoft, NVIDIA, CrowdStrike, JPMorganChase, and the Linux Foundation. By June 2, 2026 it expanded to ~150 more orgs in 15+ countries, reporting 10,000+ high/critical flaws found, including a 27-year-old OpenBSD bug.

SRCanthropic.com/glasswing·cnbc.com
2026.06.12
U.S. GOV DIRECTIVE
RestrictedAnthropic Statement

Fable 5 and Mythos 5 yanked offline

Citing national-security authorities, the U.S. government issued an export-control directive suspending all access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5 by any foreign national, anywhere, including Anthropic's own foreign-national staff, forcing Anthropic to disable the models for all customers. The stated trigger was a believed jailbreak. Anthropic complied but openly disagreed, noting the same capability is "widely available from other models (including OpenAI's GPT-5.5)."

> INTERCEPT // Anthropic statement2026.06.12
"If this standard was applied across the industry, we believe it would essentially halt all new model deployments for all frontier model providers."
SRCanthropic.com/fable-mythos-access
2026.06.26
OPENAI
Restricted

OpenAI gates GPT-5.6 to "trusted partners"

Two weeks later OpenAI announced GPT-5.6 "Sol," plus "Terra" and "Luna," and said it was complying with a government request to limit the rollout to a small set of government-approved partners (~20 customers), while stating it doesn't believe this access process "should become the long-term default." CNBC tied it directly to the Anthropic suspension. Restoration status of all of these remained unresolved.

Where the record is soft.

ANECDOTE

The individual "psychosis" stories are largely self-reported via family and Reddit. CNN itself noted it "has not independently confirmed" them. Treat the phenomenon and the pattern as documented, not each case as adjudicated fact.

NOT CLINICAL

"Chatbot psychosis" is not a DSM/ICD diagnosis. Psychiatrists are divided, and some argue the label fixates on delusion while ignoring other features of psychosis.

FAST-MOVING

The 2026 items, Mythos/Fable, GPT-5.6, the export directive, are verified against primary statements and major outlets but remain fluid; restoration was pending as of late June 2026.

CONTESTED

Whether the 2023 extinction letters were sincere risk communication or hype and regulatory capture is itself the dispute. This dossier presents the statements and the critiques rather than resolving motive.
