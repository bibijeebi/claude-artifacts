Tool Sort & Gap Checklist

Lay this on the table. Sort your pile, place each tool by its row. Check the box if you have it. An empty box is a gap, that's your shopping list.

SAE vs METRIC: Fraction stamped (e.g. 9/16) = SAE/inch.   Plain number, sometimes "MM" (e.g. 14) = Metric.   DRIVE SIZE = the square hole on the socket's back. Match it to your ratchet.

Misc / Unsorted

Anything that didn't fit a group: nut drivers, Torx, line wrenches, crowfoot sockets, impact sockets (black), pry bars, files, etc. List them so nothing gets lost.

ItemCountKeep / Toss

After sorting: Count the checked boxes in each section, that's the organizer capacity you buy. Every empty box is a shopping list item. Buy spares of 10mm metric and 1/2" SAE on principle.
