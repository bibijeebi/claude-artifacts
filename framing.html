    how a house carries itself

    Framing is a load path

    A frame's whole job is to carry weight to the ground and stay rigid in wind. Every member is either passing load down or holding the shape.

    Platform framing, bottom to top:
    foundation → pressure-treated sill plate (anchor-bolted) → floor joists + subfloor (the platform) → stud walls built flat and tilted up → roof, stick-framed rafters or engineered trusses.

    Load runs the other way: roof → walls → floor → foundation → soil.

    Nine builds, bottom to top: the floor platform, a solid wall, a window wall, a door wall, a corner and partition tee, a rafter roof, a truss roof, a hip and valley roof, and a stair flight. Place every member yourself.
