How it works

A house frame has one job: carry weight to the ground and stay rigid in wind. The order is always the same. Foundation → pressure-treated sill → floor platform → walls → roof. Load runs the other way: roof to walls to floor to foundation to soil. Every stud, header, jack and chord exists to pass that load down or hold the shape.

Learn mode gives you part labels, hints, and a full explanation of every piece as you place it. A wrong spot tells you what actually belongs there. Test mode hides the labels and scores you on accuracy and time, with stars and saved best runs, so you can drill until it's automatic.

Schematic teaching views with nominal dimensions. Verify spans, headers, and tie requirements against code span tables before you cut anything.
