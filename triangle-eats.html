Triangle Eats Field Guide

Six regional sweeps across Raleigh and every suburb in the circle, filtered for the places you two keep liking: owner-run, unfussy, and better than they look. Your own verdicts are on every place you've been.

Bottom line: Garibaldi is still the benchmark. The strongest untested bets are Metro 8 Steakhouse (Durham), Prime Barbecue (Knightdale, Michelin Bib Gourmand), Little Portugal (Fuquay), Vic's Italian (downtown Raleigh) and Gabriella's (Benson). For Sunday brunch, go back to Guglhupf or try Vin Rouge.

How to read this

Miles are estimated driving distance from three bases: Home (Eagle Chase Dr, Willow Spring), Ronnie (Carpe Diem Ln, south Raleigh) and Josh (Bainbridge Cary, Chapel Hill Rd). They come from straight-line distance plus 25%, so treat them as a sorting aid and check Maps for drive time. Ratings name their source: G = Google, OT = OpenTable, RJ = Restaurantji (an aggregator; its counts run lower than Google's). Hours came from listings in October 2026 and drift, so call before a long drive. Gold border = top pick. Photos come from each restaurant's own website; places without a usable photo show their name instead.
