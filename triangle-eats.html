Triangle Eats Field Guide

Six regional sweeps across Raleigh and every suburb in the circle, filtered for the places you two keep liking: owner-run, unfussy, and better than they look.

Bottom line: the strongest new bets are Metro 8 Steakhouse (Durham), Prime Barbecue (Knightdale, Michelin Bib Gourmand), Little Portugal (Fuquay), Vic's Italian (downtown Raleigh) and Gabriella's (Benson). For Sunday brunch: Guglhupf again, Vin Rouge, or The Depot on First.
