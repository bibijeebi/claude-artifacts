Clemson NC State UW-Madison

Executive Summary

The Bottom Line

NC State offers the best combination of cost and reputation if NC residency is maintained. UW-Madison becomes optimal if relocating to Wisconsin first. Clemson is a high-risk/high-reward wildcard with unknown selectivity as a brand-new program.

Difficulty Ranking

1. Clemson — New program, ~80 seats, unknown selectivity
2. UW-Madison — ~5% acceptance, 34 non-res seats
3. NC State — ~5% acceptance, only 25 non-res seats

Cost Ranking (4-Year)

Cheapest: NC State in-state ~$176k
Mid-range: UW-Madison in-state ~$197k
Most Expensive: Clemson OOS ~$333k

Key Insight

Residency status is everything. As a non-resident, you're competing for 25-34 seats against 1,500+ applicants. Establish residency before applying whenever possible.

🎯 Strategic Verdict

For highest probability of acceptance with lowest risk: NC State as NC resident. For best positioning if relocating to Wisconsin: UW-Madison after establishing WI residency (12 months). For a potential easier path with higher risk: Clemson (but only if comfortable with provisional accreditation).

Strategic Analysis

Recommendation Scenarios

Choose your path based on current situation and goals.

1

Stay in North Carolina

Best: NC State

Compete in the 80% resident pool with the lowest tuition in the country. Top-5 national ranking with proximity to current home. Raleigh BAH is solid at ~$1,827/month.

2

Relocate to Wisconsin First

Best: UW-Madison

Establish WI residency (12 months) before applying. Compete in ~62-seat resident pool instead of 34-seat non-resident pool. Excellent dairy/food animal training. GRE required—factor in prep time.

3

Maximize Admission Odds (Risk Tolerant)

Consider: Clemson

New program may be less competitive initially. Scholarship support for inaugural class. Military background valued under "resiliency" factor. Risk: Provisional accreditation, unproven program.

4

Apply to Multiple Schools

Hedged Approach

Apply to NC State (resident advantage) + UW-Madison (future home state) + one safety option. Diversifies risk while maintaining strong positioning at preferred schools.
