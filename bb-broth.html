BB BrothBekah's Bone Broth

Slow-simmered 12-hour chicken stock from a Costco rotisserie carcass. Real broth. No shortcuts.

↓

It started with a shredded rotisserie chicken from Costco and Bekah deciding the carcass wasn't going in the trash. Carrots got peeled. Celery got chopped. The big pot came out. Twelve hours later, liquid gold.

What You Need

🍗
Chicken Carcass 1 rotisserie chicken, meat shredded off
🥕
Carrots Peeled, cut into chunks
🥬
Celery Same size pieces as the carrots
🧄
Garlic Cloves Whole, smashed or halved
🧅
Onion Quartered (or onion powder in a pinch)
🍃
Bay Leaves 1–2 leaves
💧
Cold Water Enough to just cover everything
⚠️ Use cold water. Not hot. Cold.

The Method

Strip the Bird

Shred all usable meat off your Costco rotisserie chicken. Set the meat aside for sandwiches, tacos, whatever. The carcass, bones, skin, cartilage, all that good stuff goes in the pot.

Prep the Vegetables

Peel and chop your carrots into rough chunks. Cut celery to roughly the same size. Smash a few garlic cloves. Quarter an onion if you've got one. If not, onion powder works.

Load the Pot

Carcass in first. Vegetables around it. Toss in your bay leaves and garlic. Fill with cold water until everything is just barely covered. No more.

Bring to a Boil

Crank the heat. Once you've got a rolling boil, immediately drop it down to the lowest simmer you can manage. You want gentle bubbles, not a jacuzzi.

Walk Away

Let it go. 12 hours minimum. The longer it simmers, the more gelatin and flavor you extract from those bones. Your house is going to smell incredible.

Strain & Store

Strain through a fine mesh sieve. Discard the solids. Let the broth cool, then pour into freezer molds. You've now got homemade stock on demand.

⏱️

12+ Hours

Low and slow. That's the whole secret. The collagen breaks down, the marrow renders out, and you end up with stock that gels in the fridge. That's how you know it's real.

Bekah's Tips

❄️

Always start with cold water. Hot water can seal the outside of bones too quickly and prevent full extraction. Cold water draws out more collagen and flavor as it slowly heats.

🧊

Freeze in big molds. Ice cube trays work for small portions, but grab some silicone molds for larger blocks. One block = one pot of soup.

🥇

The jiggle test. Good broth will solidify in the fridge like Jell-O. That's pure gelatin from the bones. If it jiggles, you nailed it.

♻️

Don't waste the bird. A $5 Costco rotisserie gives you dinner AND stock. That's two meals and a freezer full of broth from one chicken.
