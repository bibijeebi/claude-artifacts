What the pictures say

Age. Eyes open, ears standing, full coats, still mostly sleeping in a pile. That's the 2 to 3 week window. Expect them to start climbing out of the bed and nibbling hay within days if they aren't already.
Coat genetics, in plain terms. Three broken (spotted) kits plus two ruby-eyed whites plus a chestnut out of one litter means at least one parent carries the broken gene and both carry the albino (REW) gene. That's why you got such a spread from one pairing.
Confidence. Kits 1, 2, 5, 6, 7, 8 are unambiguous. The two solid blacks (3, 4) are only separable by which video they were in; if you handle them side by side, the one that looks greyer is Kit 4. Sexes can't be called from these photos; check at 4+ weeks.
