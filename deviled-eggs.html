📝 What You Need

For the Eggs

  • 15 eggs
  • 1 cup water
  • Big bowl of ice water (ready before eggs finish)

For the Filling

  • Hellmann's Real Mayonnaise
  • Grey Poupon Dijon Mustard
  • Worcestershire Sauce
  • Sweet pickle juice (just the liquid!)
  • Salt & pepper

Toppings

  • Cape Fear Pirate Candy Jalapeños 🌶️
  • Paprika (optional, for classic look)

🥚 Phase 1: Hard-Boiled Eggs

  1. Add 1 cup water to Instant Pot
  2. Place eggs on trivet (stacking is fine)
  3. Seal lid, turn valve to SEALING
  4. Press Pressure Cook → High → 5 minutes
  5. Walk away! Takes ~10 min to pressurize, then counts down
⚠️ When it beeps: DO NOT TOUCH for 5 minutes! This is the natural release.
  1. After 5 min, turn valve to venting to release remaining pressure
  2. Transfer eggs immediately to ice bath for 5 minutes
  3. Peel under cold running water if shells stick
💡 Pro tip: Roll peeled egg on counter to crack shell evenly before peeling. Older eggs (1-2 weeks) peel easier than fresh.

😈 Phase 2: Devil Those Eggs

  1. Slice all 15 eggs in half lengthwise
  2. Pop yolks into a bowl, arrange whites on a plate
  3. Mash yolks with a fork until smooth (no chunks!)

Mix Into Yolks (for 15 eggs / 30 halves):

  • 5-6 tbsp Hellmann's mayo
  • 1.5 tsp Grey Poupon
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire
  • 1-2 tsp sweet pickle juice
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  1. Taste the filling and adjust seasonings
  2. Spoon or pipe filling back into whites
  3. Top with candied jalapeños right before serving
🌶️ Candied jalapeños go on TOP, not mixed in!

✨ Final Tips

  • Add jalapeños right before serving so they stay crispy
  • Can be made a day ahead (store covered, add toppings when serving)
  • Dust with paprika for that classic look
  • Refrigerate until ready to serve
