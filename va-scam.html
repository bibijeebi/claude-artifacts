That VA Text Is a Scam

Here's what's actually happening and why you keep getting this garbage

📱 The Text You Got

+1 (628) 277-5677

"Hey Amanda, It's Patrick. I'm checking back on your VA IRRRL benefit. We can text if its easier, is your current VA int-rate still above 6?"

  • Cold-texting about your mortgage — Legit lenders don't do this
  • 628 area code — San Francisco, commonly spoofed by robodialers
  • 6:42 AM follow-up — Automated drip campaign, not a real person
  • "Patrick from VA rate reduction team" — Vague, unofficial-sounding
  • Your phone flagged it as spam — Trust your phone

🔄 How This Machine Works

The VA IRRRL (Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan) is a real program. These scammers exploit it.

1 Data brokers compile lists of veterans with VA loans from public records
↓
2 Lists get sold to mortgage boiler rooms (often multiple times)
↓
3 They blast automated texts to thousands of people
↓
4 Anyone who responds gets pressured into a garbage refi with junk fees

They don't actually know your loan details. They just know your name + "probably has a VA mortgage." It's pure volume: text 10,000 people, 50 respond, 5 close, profit.

📬 The Mail Is the Same Shit

Those official-looking letters? Same playbook, different channel.

Fake urgency

"FINAL NOTICE" or "TIME-SENSITIVE DOCUMENT" — It's not.

Fake officiality

Looks like it's from the VA or your lender — It's not. Check the return address.

Scary numbers

"Your rate could drop from 6.5% to 4.9%!" — Made up. They don't know your rate.

Same goal: get you to call them so they can pressure you into a refi that benefits them, not you. The letters cost more to send, so they look more legit. They're not.

🤔 Is This Dangerous?

No. This isn't identity theft or a targeted attack. It's bottom-feeder mortgage marketing. They're not going to steal your house or your identity from this. They're just annoying and predatory.

The worst case if you engage: you get talked into a refinance with bad terms and unnecessary fees. That can cost you thousands, but it's financial predation, not fraud in the criminal sense.

✅ What To Do

  • Block the number — Don't reply, even to say "stop"
  • Trash the mail — Don't call the numbers on it
  • If you actually want to refi — Go through your current loan servicer or a credit union directly, never through cold solicitations
  • Optional: Report to FTC at reportfraud.ftc.gov

This is not a threat. It's just garbage marketing.

Block. Delete. Move on.
