The Trillion-Dollar Question

They say it's not regime change. But they killed the Supreme Leader and Trump is telling Iranians "now is your time."

Hegseth's own words: "This is not a so-called regime change war, but the regime sure did change."

Whether sustained air strikes can actually collapse a government without boots on the ground is the central debate. Historically, it doesn't work. Multiple analysts are already pointing out that airpower alone has never toppled a regime. The administration is explicitly ruling out ground forces and nation-building — but the stated political end state (Iranian people seizing their own government) requires something air strikes can't deliver on their own.

The gap between the military objectives (missiles, navy, nukes) and the political objective (regime falls from within) is where this thing either succeeds or turns into something nobody planned for.
