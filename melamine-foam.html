What Is Melamine Foam?

Melamine foam is a thermoset plastic foam made from melamine resin. Developed in the 1990s by BASF for insulation and soundproofing, someone eventually discovered its remarkable cleaning properties. Mr. Clean licensed it in 2003, slapped a bald mascot on the package, and charged a premium ever since.

The generics come from the same manufacturers producing the same material to the same specifications. It's a commodity chemical product, not a proprietary formula.

How It Works

1

Micro-Abrasive Structure

The foam has a unique open-cell structure that acts like ultra-fine sandpaper, far finer than anything visible to the naked eye.

2

Mechanical Cleaning

When wet and rubbed, it physically scrapes away the top layer of grime. It's not chemical cleaning, it's microscopic sanding.

3

Self-Sacrificing

The foam disintegrates as you use it because it's literally abrading itself along with the surface. This is normal and expected.

Usage Guide

Great For

  • Scuff marks on walls & floors
  • Soap scum in bathrooms
  • Water stains & mineral deposits
  • Permanent marker on hard surfaces
  • Grime on baseboards & trim
  • Sneaker soles (white rubber)
  • Keyboard & mouse gunk
  • Register covers & grilles

Never Use On

  • Glossy or painted surfaces
  • Car paint (causes swirl marks)
  • Wood finishes & lacquer
  • Stainless steel
  • Non-stick cookware
  • Skin (causes micro-abrasions)
  • Screens of any kind
  • Anything you wouldn't sand
