🐱 MEET ETHEL 🐱

The Real One Behind The Meme

Ethel is a blind rescue cat with dwarfism, adopted in 2018 from the Cat Welfare Association in Ohio. She was born with a degenerative eye disease and loves to spin in circles at mealtime.

Her owner Donna fell in love at first sight. Nobody else wanted to adopt her. Now she's an international internet superstar.

📅 THE OIIA TIMELINE 📅

2018

A blind, chonky cat with dwarfism finds her forever home from the Cat Welfare Association in Ohio. Nobody else wanted her. Love at first sight.

The Photo That Started It All
Ethel's photo gets posted to the Facebook group "Cats On Catnip Group". Latin American meme creators pick it up as "Ai Dise Gratis".
APRIL 12, 2023
The Birth of OIIA
Bilibili user 黑瘦的鱼头 uploads a remix of the "Oo Ee A E A" sound with a spinning 3D cat model. 12+ million views. The brainrot begins.

Why Do Indonesians Call It "Kucing Akmal"?

In Indonesia, the meme is known as "Kucing Akmal" (Akmal's Cat). "Akmal" is just a common Indonesian name that got randomly attached to the meme – similar to how generic names like "Budi" or "Joko" become meme fodder in Indonesian internet culture.

The cat's real name was never Akmal. It's Ethel. But the Indonesian internet does what it wants. 🤷
