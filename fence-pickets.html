Function In Disguise

BLUF. Two unrelated things get conflated. Individual picket tops (dog-ear, gothic point) are cheap because they're standard stock and they shed water off end grain — flat-top pickets rot from the top down and you'll regret them. Section-level scallops (curve across a bay between posts) are pure aesthetics that add 30-60 min per bay. Skip if utility, build if curb appeal.

The Mechanism: Why End Grain Matters

Wood is a bundle of straws. Cut perpendicular to the grain (the top of a picket) and you've got open-ended straws facing the sky. That's the kill mechanism for flat tops.

FLAT TOP — Capillary Action

  • End grain = exposed straws
  • Water wicks vertically down fibers
  • Wet wood swells, dries, splits
  • Splits trap more water → rot
  • Failure starts at the top, eats down

ANGLED TOP — Sheet Flow

  • Angled face directs gravity
  • Water leaves before soaking in
  • End grain points sideways = less exposure
  • Top stays drier between rains
  • 2-4× longer service life vs flat

Why people actually do it

Real reasons, in honesty order:

  • 01Curb appeal. Scalloped reads as "intentional residential," flat reads as "construction site." Realtors notice.
  • 02Privacy where you need it. Tall at the posts (where neighbors look in at angles), shorter in the middle (where nobody's eye-level).
  • 03Forgives mistakes. A curve hides an off-by-quarter-inch picket. A flat line broadcasts every error.
  • 04Visual rhythm. Long fence runs become monotonous. The curve gives the eye something to land on every 8 ft.

Pro tip — homestead pragmatist

For your application (Stamey's Walk perimeter, animal containment, not show-piece), buy pre-cut dog-ear cedar pickets. End grain is already handled. Skip scalloping unless you're staring at it from your kitchen window every morning. The single highest-leverage upgrade isn't the top profile — it's a 2x4 cedar cap rail running across all picket tops. It adds rigidity, hides any top misalignment, protects every picket's end grain, and makes the fence read as "finished" instead of "DIY." Best case lifespan with cedar + cap rail + ground gap: 20–25 yrs. Without cap rail: 12–15.
