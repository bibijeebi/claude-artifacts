Nick Fuentes

Nicholas Joseph Fuentes
b. Aug 18, 1998La Grange Park, ILAge 27Groyper Leader

Nick Fuentes is the leader of the Groyper movement and host of the nightly livestream America First, making him arguably the most visible figure in the post-2019 American far right under 30. He attended Boston University briefly before dropping out to pursue political commentary full-time.

Rise to Prominence

Fuentes first gained attention attending the 2017 Unite the Right rally in Charlottesville as a teenager. His breakthrough came during the 2019 "Groyper War," a coordinated trolling campaign where his followers hijacked Turning Point USA campus events, pressing Charlie Kirk on Israel, immigration, and social conservatism. The campaign established Fuentes as the leader of a youth-oriented dissident right faction positioned as more radical than mainstream conservatism but wrapped in irony and meme culture.

Ideology

Fuentes identifies as a Christian nationalist and Catholic traditionalist. He promotes white nationalism, antisemitism (including Holocaust questioning), opposition to immigration, anti-feminism, and hostility toward LGBTQ rights. He publicly identifies as a "proud incel" and posted "Your body, my choice. Forever" after the November 2024 election (100M+ views).

Key Events

In November 2022, Fuentes and Kanye West dined with Donald Trump at Mar-a-Lago, generating massive controversy. By 2025, he had pivoted to calling Trump a "scam artist" while gaining mainstream exposure through appearances on Patrick Bet-David, Bradley Martyn, and most significantly a Tucker Carlson interview (October 2025).

Deplatforming: Banned from virtually every major platform and payment processor. Placed on federal no-fly list. Banned from Airbnb. Twitter/X reinstated by Elon Musk (May 2024), now 1M+ followers. Senate resolution condemning Fuentes introduced November 2025.

Conservative writer Rod Dreher reported estimates that 30-40% of Republican staffers under 30 in Washington are Groypers, though this is disputed. Founded AFPAC (America First Political Action Conference) as a white nationalist alternative to CPAC.
