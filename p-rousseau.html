Thomas Rousseau

Thomas Ryan Rousseau
b. 1998Coppell (Dallas), TXAge ~27Patriot Front Founder

Thomas Rousseau is the founder and sole leader of Patriot Front, America's most prolific white nationalist propaganda organization, which he created as a teenager in the direct aftermath of the 2017 Charlottesville rally.

Early Radicalization

Rousseau attended Coppell High School in the Dallas suburbs, where he wrote for the school newspaper The Sidekick (conservative opinion columns on campus carry, bathroom bills, Trump). The FBI began monitoring Rousseau in May 2017, while he was still a high school student distributing white nationalist flyers on Dallas-area college campuses.

Vanguard America → Patriot Front

As a teenager, Rousseau joined Vanguard America (VA), rapidly seizing control of its website and Discord after feuding with VA's "Commander." He led VA's contingent at the Unite the Right rally, where VA became "radioactive" after James Alex Fields Jr., who had rallied with VA, drove his car into counter-protesters and killed Heather Heyer. Within weeks, Rousseau rebranded VA's remnants as Patriot Front: swapping black-and-white neo-Nazi imagery for red-white-and-blue Americana, Betsy Ross flags, and bald eagles, while retaining the fasces symbol and identical ideology.

Outsized impact: Despite only ~200 members (Rousseau complained about a "220's to 230's membership rut"), Patriot Front generated 82% of all white supremacist propaganda distribution incidents in the US in 2021, nearly 4,000 incidents across every continental state (ADL). Members are required to purchase propaganda materials from the group and meet mandatory distribution quotas.
Arrests & Legal Fallout

Arrested in 2020 (stickers on a Texas courthouse); June 2022 in Coeur d'Alene, Idaho (31 members in a U-Haul plotting to disrupt an LGBT Pride event); 2024 (Virginia warrant for "burning an object with intent to intimidate," a tiki torch at Charlottesville). A 2022 Boston march resulted in the assault of Charles Murrell III, a Black man, leading to a $2.7 million civil judgment. A 2022 car crash killed one member and seriously injured Rousseau (emergency surgery).

Rousseau is the only Patriot Front figure whose identity is publicly known. He mandates strict anonymity for all other members and demands total commitment: "Activities in the org aren't a pick-and-choose buffet. You're in, or you're out."
