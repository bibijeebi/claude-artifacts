A Field Guide

The Yuppie
& the polaroid

aspirational WASP, two ways
§ 01 — DEFINITION

What it is.

Yuppie is shorthand for young urban professional, but the word does heavier lifting than that. It names an aspirational WASP / Ivy-prep visual language. A small, specific universe of cream polos and oxblood leather, of summer houses and signet rings and quiet money. The look has two distinct flavors and they don't mix the way you'd think.

"It is the manner in which one wears the polo, not the polo itself."

One flavor was built in the 1980s, when MBAs and corporate raiders made the suit and the suspenders into a uniform of ambition. The other was resurrected two decades later by a generation of indie kids who treated faded polaroids of country clubs as a kind of melancholy nostalgia. The Vampire Weekend Contra cover sits squarely in flavor two.

§ 02 — THE TWO FLAVORS

Power, ⚜ & nostalgia.

Reagan-era · Materialism · Ambition

180s Power Yuppie

The Patrick Bateman flavor. Pinstripes, suspenders, slicked hair, BMW keys, Filofax. Cold and ambitious and coke-fueled. References: Wall Street, American Psycho, The Bonfire of the Vanities. The villain of every 80s movie.

Wears: Brooks Brothers · Drinks: Macallan, neat · Drives: BMW 3-series

Late-aughts revival · Nostalgia · Irony

2Contra-Era Indie Yuppie

The flavor in your screenshot. Faded polaroid prep nostalgia, ironic Columbia worship, soft and warm and summer-y. Vampire Weekend, Whit Stillman, the late-aughts blogosphere of The Sartorialist. The Bateman shadow turned wistful.

Wears: Polo by Ralph Lauren · Drinks: G&T · Drives: a Volvo wagon (used)

§ 05 — THE VOCABULARY

Words to drop, casually.

These are the dog-whistles. None of them are deployed seriously. The whole register is performed slightly tongue-in-cheek, the way someone knows they're playing a part.

"old money"quiet wealth
"summering"verb only
"trust fund"self-aware
"boarding school"Andover, Exeter
"the Cape"never "Cape Cod"
"prep"noun, verb, slur
"WASP"used proudly
"Locust Valley lockjaw"the accent
"Ivy"league, league
"the club"tennis or country
§ 06 — THE CANON

What to read & watch.

  • The Official Preppy Handbook— Lisa Birnbach, 1980 The ironic field manual. Half satire, half instruction, fully canonical.
  • Take Ivy— Hayashida et al., 1965 Japanese photographers documenting Ivy League menswear. The visual bible.
  • Metropolitan— Whit Stillman, 1990 Debutante balls. The whole Stillman trilogy is required.
  • American Psycho— Bret Easton Ellis, 1991 The dark mirror. Wall Street yuppie as serial killer.
  • Contra— Vampire Weekend, 2010 The whole Vampire Weekend discography, but this one most of all.
  • Gossip Girl— CW, 2007–12 The decadent teen flavor. Upper East Side, paparazzi-glossed.
§ 07 — IF YOU'RE GENERATING IMAGES

The polaroid prompt.

Drop this into any image model when you want the Contra-era flavor. Swap out the subject; keep the lighting and film cues intact, that's where the aesthetic actually lives.

"Polaroid photograph, late 1980s, [subject], wearing a cream Ralph Lauren polo with slight popped collar, tousled hair, direct flash photography, soft natural makeup, against a beige wall or Cape Cod summer house interior, warm yellow film cast, slight overexposure, soft focus, late-80s polaroid grain, white polaroid border with handwritten date in ballpoint, dreamy nostalgic Americana."

Pro tip: the magic is in warm yellow film cast, direct flash, and soft focus. Take any of those out and the whole thing reads as cosplay.
