Go see Primetime.

BLUF: Primetime, 5:30 or 8:15 at Fenton. Pattinson as Chris Hansen, A24, Ari Aster producing, deadpan dark satire, 89% RT. Runner-up is the Pan's Labyrinth 20th anniversary re-release at Regal. No new Wes Anderson this week.

Thin slate otherwise. The two biggest new openings are a Colleen Hoover adaptation (Verity, 41% RT, C+ CinemaScore) and a jump-scare horror movie with no reviews yet. Primetime is in its third weekend, so this is probably the last weekend it has good evening times everywhere.

Ranked

Primetime PICK

1h50m · R · Lance Oppenheim · A24, produced by Ari Aster

Robert Pattinson as Chris Hansen in 2006, building To Catch a Predator, until the show eats itself.

RT 89% · Metacritic 69 · IMDb 7.0 · Letterboxd 3.8 · CinemaScore n/a · 3rd weekend

Dead center of the lane. Deadpan dark comedy with horror-style shock cuts, aimed at reality TV and gotcha journalism. Boston Globe compares it to To Die For and Nightcrawler. Main knock: gets busy and loses steam in the last act, and the subject matter is grim. Not a cute date movie, a good one.

Pan's Labyrinth (20th anniversary) RUNNER-UP

1h58m · R · Guillermo del Toro · Spanish, subtitled

1944 Spain. A girl escapes her fascist stepfather into a fairy-tale underworld that is worse.

RT 95% / 91% audience · Metacritic 98

Re-release, not a remake, so it clears the filter. Elevated dark fantasy-horror with a real auteur behind it. If you've only seen it on a TV, this is the better night than most of the new stuff. Limited showings, mostly Regal.

Digger WILDCARD

2h9m · R · Alejandro G. Iñárritu

Tom Cruise in prosthetics as an oil tycoon trying to undo a global catastrophe he caused.

RT 52% / 71% audience · CinemaScore C+ · opened $8M on a reported $130M+ budget

On paper it's exactly your kind of swing: original, auteur, Strangelove-style satire. In practice critics split hard. IndieWire calls it detached, BBC loved it as a love-it-or-hate-it folly. There's a 10-minute climate monologue everyone mentions. Go only if you want to argue about it on the drive home. It'll lose screens fast.

The History of Concrete

1h42m · John Wilson (How To with John Wilson)

Wilson takes a Hallmark-movie writing workshop and tries to use it to pitch a documentary about concrete.

RT 97% / 75% audience · Metacritic 81 · Letterboxd 4.0

Funniest thing playing, and it's a doc. Only at Alamo Raleigh: Fri 6:00, Sat 7:00 and 10:00. Strong pick if you've seen Primetime.

Heart of the Beast

1h41m · PG-13 · David Ayer

Retired Special Forces vet (Brad Pitt) and his combat dog survive a plane crash in the Alaska bush.

RT 85% / 94% audience · Metacritic 69 · CinemaScore A-

The original crowd-pleaser of the slate. Audiences love it. It's a sincere survival-and-dog movie, no directorial voice to speak of. Fine if Bekah wants the easy one. Expect to cry about the dog.

Other Mommy

1h33m · R · Rob Savage · Jessica Chastain · prod. James Wan

A monster poses as a kid's mother. Opens this Friday.

No critic scores posted yet

Savage says it has about 50 jump scares. That's the Blumhouse lane, not the Eggers/Aster lane. Skip unless reviews surprise.
