Three answers the WGU key gets "wrong" on purpose

Memorize these as keyed, not as real law: the Fair Credit Reporting Act covers medical records; the duty to suggest a non-obvious accommodation is the employee's; an ADEA defense is employing fewer than 10 people (real threshold is 20). They are PAM items in this bank and are keyed the WGU way.

Labor law in three acts

Wagner 1935 (NLRA): Workers get rights, NLRB is born. Taft-Hartley 1947 (LMRA): Tames the unions, adds union ULPs, bans the closed shop, creates the FMCS, allows right-to-work, 80-day national emergency injunction. Landrum-Griffin 1959 (LMRDA): Looks inside the union, member bill of rights, officer elections every 3 years, financial reporting. W-T-L: Workers, Tame, Look. NLRB is the referee (elections, ULP charges); FMCS is the marriage counselor (deadlocks, mediation, never rules on anything). At a genuine impasse the lawful moves are strike, lockout, or the employer implementing its last offer.

Harassment liability in one line each

Supervisor plus tangible action (fired, demoted, not promoted) = strict liability, no defense. Supervisor, no tangible action = Faragher/Ellerth defense: employer took reasonable care and the employee unreasonably skipped the complaint process. Coworker or customer = liable only if the employer knew or should have known and did nothing. Race harassment liability comes from two kinds of conduct: the employer's own policy or practice, and tolerating a hostile environment it knew about.

Numbers that get tested

Employer size: Title VII, ADA, GINA 15 · ADEA 20 · FMLA 50 within 75 miles · WARN 100 · COBRA 20 · IRCA citizenship-status rule 4. EEOC charge 180 days (300 in a deferral state), right-to-sue then 90 days to file. Four-fifths rule: selection rate under 80% of the top group. FMLA: 12 months and 1,250 hours, 12 weeks, 26 for a servicemember caregiver, certification back in 15 days. COBRA 18 months (termination, hours cut), 29 (disability), 36 (divorce, death). WARN 60 days notice, plant closing 50+ jobs, mass layoff 500 or 50 at 33%. I-9 Section 2 within 3 business days, keep 3 years after hire or 1 year after termination. OSHA: fatality reported in 8 hours, amputation or hospitalization in 24, 300A posted Feb 1 to Apr 30, retaliation complaint within 30 days. FLSA: overtime over 40, tipped cash wage $2.13, youth wage $4.25 for 90 days. OWBPA waiver 21 days to consider, 7 to revoke (45 days for a group). NLRB: 30% showing of interest, majority of votes cast wins, certification bar 12 months.
