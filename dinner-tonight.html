The Main Event

Chicken Quesadillas

~300g shredded chicken, Costco tortillas, cheddar block + parm block. Feeds two generously.

Crispy, cheesy, golden perfection

Season the Chicken

Toss the 300g shredded chicken in a bowl with these spices and a squeeze of lime juice:

Cumin Chili Powder Garlic Powder Smoked Paprika Salt Onion Powder Cayenne (optional) Lime juice

Go about 1 tsp each on the key spices, half tsp on the rest. Taste as you go.

Prep the Cheese

Shred the cheddar block, that's your main cheese. Grate some parmesan separately for the outside crust trick (see pro tip below).

Cook

  1. Heat the skillet to medium

    Cast iron preferred. Let it get properly hot before anything goes in.

  2. Lay down a tortilla, cheese first

    Spread shredded cheddar on one half of the tortilla. This is your glue layer.

  3. Add the seasoned chicken

    Layer the chicken on top of the cheese. Don't overstuff, you need it to fold clean.

  4. More cheese, then fold

    Another layer of cheddar on top of the chicken, then fold the tortilla in half. Press gently with spatula.

  5. Cook 2-3 min per side

    Golden and crispy on the outside, cheese fully melted inside. Don't rush it, medium heat is your friend.

  6. Cut into triangles, serve

    Let it rest 30 seconds so the cheese sets slightly, then cut. Serve with lime wedges.

🔥 Pro tip: Sprinkle grated parmesan directly on the outside of the tortilla right before it hits the pan. It creates a crispy parm crust that's absolutely insane. Cheese on both sides of the chicken = cheese glue that holds everything together.
