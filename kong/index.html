Reference

Every shot, in order
  1. Vial from fridge (35–45°F, never frozen). Confirm vial number. Roll gently, don't shake.
  2. Alcohol on the stopper. Fresh syringe, draw exact dose. Never reuse a syringe.
  3. Tent loose skin over the shoulders or mid-back. Move the spot each time.
  4. Needle in, pull back the plunger. Blood in the hub: pull out, new spot.
  5. No blood: inject. Vial back in the fridge.
  6. Watch him 30 minutes. Only dose when someone's home for the full window.
  7. Tell Claude: shot number, time, spot, any reaction.
Reactions
  • Normal: mild redness or itching at the site.
  • Call PetSound before next dose: noticeably itchier for a day or two after.
  • Emergency vet now: vomiting, diarrhea, hives, facial swelling, labored breathing, weakness or collapse.
Missed dose, refills, vial switches
  • Missed or late dose: don't double up. Call PetSound for how to restart, especially during build-up.
  • Shot #6 drops to 0.2 mL on purpose. Vial 2 is 10× stronger than Vial 1 and darker. Retire Vial 1 after #5 even with ~2.5 mL left.
  • Order the refill at ~3/4 of Vial 2 used (around shot #13–14). Custom mixed, so order early.
  • First dose from any new vial is 0.5 mL, then back to 1.0 mL.
  • Expect 4–6 months before improvement, best results at 10–12 months.
