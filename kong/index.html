    How to

    Every shot, in order
    1. Vial from the fridge (35–45°F, never frozen). Confirm the vial number. Roll gently, don't shake.
    2. Alcohol on the stopper. Fresh syringe, draw the exact dose. Never reuse a syringe.
    3. Tent loose skin over the shoulder. Use the other shoulder from last time.
    4. Needle in, pull back the plunger. Blood in the hub: pull out, new spot.
    5. No blood: inject. Vial back in the fridge.
    6. Watch him 30 minutes. Only dose when someone's home for the full window.
    7. Tell Claude the shot number, time, shoulder, and any reaction.
    Reactions
    • Normal: mild redness or itching at the site.
    • Call PetSound before the next dose: noticeably itchier for a day or two after.
    • Emergency vet now: vomiting, diarrhea, hives, facial swelling, labored breathing, weakness or collapse.
    • Benadryl on hand: 25 mg tablets, one for a mild reaction, two if it's bad.
    Missed doses, refills, vial switches
    • Missed or late dose: don't double up. Call PetSound for how to restart, especially during build-up.
    • Shot 6 drops to 0.2 mL on purpose. Vial 2 is 10× stronger than Vial 1 and darker. Retire Vial 1 after shot 5 even with ~2.5 mL left.
    • Order the refill at about 3/4 of Vial 2 used (around shot 13–14). It's custom mixed, so order early.
    • First dose from any new vial is 0.5 mL, then back to 1.0 mL.
    • Expect 4–6 months before improvement, best results at 10–12 months.
