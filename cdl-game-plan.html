EXIT 02

The Step-by-Step

Seven phases, in order. Don't skip ahead. The DOT physical is the bottleneck — knock it out first.

01
DOT Physical
Week 1 · ~$75-150

A federally-mandated medical exam by a National Registry-certified examiner. Required before the CLP can be issued. Find an examiner at nationalregistry.fmcsa.dot.gov by entering zip 64752 — there will be options in Butler, Nevada, Fort Scott, and Pittsburg.

Bring: Photo ID, list of current medications, glasses or contacts.

Outcome: A "Medical Examiner's Certificate" (DOT med card) valid for 2 years.

Pro tip: If using the CMVOST scholarship at JCCC, the scholarship reimburses this fee. Keep the receipt.
02
Study the CDL Manual
Weeks 1-2 · Free

Free PDF at dor.mo.gov/driver-license/commercial-license/manual.html. Or grab a paper copy at the DOR.

You'll take three written tests in one sitting at the License Office. Need 80% on each:

  • General Knowledge · 50 questions
  • Combination Vehicles · 20 questions
  • Air Brakes · 25 questions

Plan on 10-15 hours of study spread over 1-2 weeks. Free practice tests at dmv-written-test.com and drivingtests.org.

03
Get the Missouri CLP
End of Week 2 · $44

Two License Office options near Hume. Call ahead to confirm CDL transactions are processed:

Bring:

  • Valid Missouri driver's license
  • DOT Medical Examiner's Certificate
  • Birth certificate or passport (proof of identity)
  • Social Security card or W-2/1099 with SSN
  • Two proofs of MO residency (utility bill, lease, bank statement)
  • $44 cash, check, or card

At the office: vision screening, three written tests on a computer terminal (~60-90 min), pay $44, walk out with a paper temporary CLP. Real card mails in 7-10 days.

The 14-day clock starts now. Federal rule: must hold the CLP at least 14 days before the skills test. Knock this out early so the hold burns down while you're applying to JCCC.
04
Apply to JCCC + CMVOST
Weeks 2-3 · Free

1. Pull your VA Certificate of Eligibility from va.gov/education/check-claim-status. If never used GI Bill before, file Form 22-1990 (takes ~30 days).

2. Email Nick Gonzalez at ngonza12@jccc.edu. Suggested message:

Hi Nick — I'm a Missouri-resident veteran interested in your 9-week weekend CDL program. I'd like to register for the next info session, lock in a cohort start date, apply for the CMVOST scholarship, and confirm you can administer the Missouri skills test on-site. Also want to confirm whether I can stack CMVOST + Chapter 33 MHA without burning entitlement. CLP and DOT physical already in hand.

3. Register for the free info session at continuinged.jccc.edu/courses/category/cdl.

4. Apply for CMVOST scholarship. Limited slots per cohort. Covers full $6,999 tuition + DOT physical reimbursement + drug screen + MVR + all testing fees. Apply the moment Nick opens the door.

05
9-Week Weekend Program
Weeks 4-12 · $0 with CMVOST

Location: JCCC CDL range, Edgerton, KS. About 70 miles / 1h 15min from Hume via US-71 N → I-49 N.

Schedule:

  • Mon-Fri (during the week): 4-8 hours of self-paced online theory. Done from home around the day job.
  • Sat-Sun (every weekend, 9 weekends): 8 AM - 5 PM at the Edgerton range. ~16-18 BTW hours per weekend. 60+ behind-the-wheel hours total.

What to expect each weekend:

  • Weeks 1-2: Heavy classroom + theory. Pre-trip inspection memorization (the #1 trip-up).
  • Weeks 3-5: Backing maneuvers (alley dock, parallel, offset). The hardest part of the test.
  • Weeks 6-8: Highway driving, city driving, route planning, real road conditions.
  • Week 9: Final review + skills test.

Bring to weekend class: CLP, MO driver's license, DOT med card, work boots, gloves, water, lunch.

Commute: Drive both days, 2.5 hrs total/day. Doable. Skip lodging in KC unless you're getting wiped out — it'd cost ~$1,200 over 9 weeks.
06
Pass the Missouri Skills Test
End of Week 12

Three test sections. Must pass all three:

  • Pre-trip inspection · Walk around the truck, identify and verbally explain ~30 components. The biggest fail point. Memorize this cold.
  • Basic control skills · Backing maneuvers on the range (straight back, offset back, alley dock).
  • Road test · 30-45 min in real traffic, including highway, intersections, and turns.
JCCC has Missouri-licensed third-party examiners on-site. Confirm with Nick before week 1 that the MO examiner will be available the day of your test. Backup: take the KS test, transfer to MO DOR (adds 1-2 weeks).
07
Issue the Missouri CDL
Week 12-13 · $83

Walk into Butler MO License Office with:

  • Skills test certificate from JCCC
  • Current CLP
  • DOT med card (still valid)
  • MO driver's license
  • $83 cash, check, or card

Outcome: A Missouri Class A CDL. Paper copy in hand same day. Plastic card mails in 7-10 days. Valid in all 50 states. Renew every 6 years.

Add endorsements at the same time ($5 each, no extra written test for some): Tanker (N) + Doubles/Triples (T) are recommended. Cheap, fast, opens up significantly more job options. HazMat (H) needs a separate written + TSA background check.

EXIT 05

Pro Tips

Things you only learn after it's too late. Read these before week 1.

DOT Physical First

It's the bottleneck. No med card = no CLP. Schedule it the same week you decide to do this.

Don't Wait on the CLP

Knock it out in week 2. The 14-day federal hold burns down while you're applying to JCCC. Saves 2 weeks total.

Memorize Pre-Trip Cold

The #1 cause of failed skills tests. Practice out loud during weekly online theory hours. Bekah-style spouse drilling works.

Confirm MO Test in Writing

Before week 1, make Nick confirm MO third-party examiner availability for your test date. Otherwise you test in KS and transfer.

Talk to Every Recruiter

Carriers come on-site weeks 7-9. Don't sign with the first one. Compare sign-on, home time, mileage rate, lane.

Apply for VA Apprenticeship Day 1

The day you start your driving job, file the apprenticeship paperwork. Extra $1,200-1,400/mo for 6-12 months.

CMVOST is First-Come

Limited slots per cohort. Apply the moment Nick opens the application window. Worst case, fall back to Chapter 33.

Cash Cushion Required

MHA hits 30-40 days after class start. First month = no VA money yet. Have $1,000-1,500 cushion before week 1 for gas.
