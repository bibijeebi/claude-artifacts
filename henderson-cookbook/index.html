Grandpa’s Soap

Grandpa was the soap maker for the family and others in the community. I have no idea how he made it. This is an old recipe, but I don't know whether it resembles his method. From my earliest recollection, lye could be bought at the store. I remember when mother was getting everything together for a kettle of soap, she purchased Red Devil Lye. Each can contained a picture of the devil that was absolutely adhorent to me. Anna Laura always placed the devils so they were grinning at me every time I went into the pantry.

When someone had all the necessary ingredients together, Grandpa went to make the soap. It was an all day affair.
