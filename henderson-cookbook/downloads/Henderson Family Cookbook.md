## Grandpa's Soap

Grandpa was the soap maker for the family and others in the community. I have no idea how he made it. This is an old recipe, but I don't know whether it resembles his method. From my earliest recollection, lye could be bought at the store. I remember when mother was getting everything together for a kettle of soap, she purchased Red Devil Lye. Each can contained a picture of the devil that was absolutely adhorent to me. Anna Laura always placed the devils so they were grinning at me every time I went into the pantry.

When someone had all the necessary ingredients together, Grandpa went to make the soap. It was an all day affair.

### Lye Soap


Drip 2 gallons rainwater through approximately 5 gallons hardwood ashes. Strain through cheese cloth. To each gallon of results, add 6 pounds cleaned grease (any kind). (To clean grease from bacon and meats that have been fried, put grease in large iron kettle with 3 to 4 times as much water, rain water preferable, and heat over fire to boiling, and then cool. After cold, lift up clean grease.) After you have lye made and grease cleaned, use 1 gallon lye water, 6 pounds grease cleaned. Boil in iron pot until rather thick. Before taking off fire, add 1/2 to 1 cup borax and 1 cup ammonia. Pour into flat box or enamel dish pan. When partially set up, cut with knife.

### Pluckettes

- 1 pkg. dry yeast
- 1/4 c. warm water
- 1 c. scalded milk
- 1/3 c. butter
- 1 tsp. salt
- 1/3 c. sugar
- 3 well beaten eggs
- 3 3/4 c. flour
- 3/4 c. sugar
- 3 tsp. cinnamon
- ground nuts (if desired)

Mix yeast in warm water. Set aside. Mix scalded milk, butter, salt and 1/3 sugar. Cool and add yeast mixture and eggs. Add flour. Makes a sticky dough. Let rise. Mix down again. Let rise. Make walnut size balls and dip in butter and roll in mixture of 3/4 cup sugar, cinnamon and ground

nuts. Pile in a greased tube pan. Let rise 30 minutes. Bake 10 minutes at 400° and 30 minutes at 350°. Turn out on plate immediately.

From: Dawn E. Hendeson, (K. B. Henderson's Sr. Daughter-In-Law)

### Salad

- 3 pkg. orange gelatin
- 3 c. boiling water
- 3 c. cold water
- 1 can crushed pineapple, drained
- 4 bananas, sliced
- 1 (6 oz.) pkg. mini marshmallows
- 1 egg
- 1 c. sugar
- 1 Tbsp. flour
- 1 c. pineapple juice
- 2 c. whipped cream
- 1 c. chopped pecans
