How the sim works

Blower curve: rated CFM at 0.5" ESP, drops off as static climbs. Coil and filter come off the top. Every duct is friction at its CFM and size (ASHRAE round duct equation), plus fittings as equivalent length. Branches share a trunk, so a fat short branch steals air from a long one. Bedrooms are scored with the door closed: a 1" undercut passes roughly 50 CFM before the room pressurizes and chokes its own supply. Jump ducts fix that. Noise flags at 700 FPM in a branch, 900 in the trunk, 500 at the filter grille face.
