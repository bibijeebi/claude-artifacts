What We're Looking For

We started with 450 names sourced from SSA top baby names (2014-2024), then filtered and categorized them based on our preferences. Here's the general framework:

✓ Classic, timeless names ✓ Must be easy to yell across a yard ✓ Celtic/Scottish heritage names ✓ Nature-inspired names (girls) ✓ Strong, clean sound
✗ Too biblical (Isaac, Abraham, Noah) ✗ Names with cultural baggage (Karen, Chad) ✗ Hard to pronounce or spell ✗ Trendy names that'll age badly
~ Shorter names preferred (soft rule) ~ Some biblical names are fine (Gabriel, Ezra)

The matches reflect these preferences pretty well. You'll notice nature names (Hazel, Violet, Lily, Juniper), Celtic names (Ronan, Beckett), French names (Camille, Eloise), and clean classics (Lucas, Ava, Gabriel).

How the App Works

Benny built a custom web app for this instead of using a spreadsheet or just talking about it. The app lives at a private URL that only Benny and Bekah can access (protected by authentication). Here's how it works:

Blind Voting

Each name shows up with three buttons: thumbs up (yes), thumbs down (no), and a shrug (maybe). Crucially, you can't see your partner's vote until after you've both voted on that name. This prevents one person's opinion from influencing the other. There's a "spoiler toggle" to reveal partner votes when you're ready.

Real-Time Sync

The app uses WebSocket connections so when one person votes, the other person's screen updates instantly. No refreshing, no waiting. A small green dot shows when you're connected live.

Categories & Filters

Names are organized into 20 categories (Classic, French, Nature, Scottish/Celtic, Modern-strong, etc.) and can be filtered by gender, category, or vote status. You can also sort by matches, maybes, or unvoted names.

Roulette Wheel

For fun, there's a spinning roulette wheel mode that pulls from the name pool with filters. It picks a random name with each spin, weighted by your current filters (like "only show unvoted girl names" or "only show mutual matches").

Bracket Elimination (Coming Soon)

Once voting is complete, the app has a head-to-head bracket mode where you compare two names at a time and pick your favorite, tournament-style, until you've narrowed down to a final ranked list.

The Bigger Picture

We're planning for a big family (6-8 kids), which is partly why we're taking the name selection seriously and building out a large pool early. We don't want to burn through all the good names on kid #1 and be stuck scrambling by kid #4.

The vasectomy reversal process is underway through the VA (separate page on that), with surgery expected in spring 2026. We're optimistic about the timeline and actively preparing on the fertility side with LH tracking and pre-conception planning.

Having a ranked, organized list of names for both boys and girls means we'll be ready no matter what, and we won't have to make a rushed decision in the delivery room. The app will grow with us, adding new names, re-running brackets, and tracking which names we've "used" as kids arrive.
