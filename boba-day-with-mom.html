Look for instant boba pearls (WuFuYuan brand or similar) in the dried goods aisle. Just boil 5 minutes, soak in syrup, done. Same delicious result, way less work!

🫧 Making Boba Pearls from Scratch

1
Make the dough (15 min)
Boil 1/2 cup water with 3 tbsp brown sugar until dissolved. Pour over 1 cup tapioca starch. Mix with chopsticks until it comes together, then knead until smooth.
2
Roll the pearls (20-30 min)
This is the fun part! Roll small pieces into tiny balls about the size of a pea. They shrink when cooked, so don't go too small. Dust with extra tapioca starch so they don't stick.
3
Cook the pearls (15-20 min)
Boil a big pot of water. Drop in the pearls, stir gently so they don't stick. They'll sink, then float. Once floating, cook 15 more minutes on medium. They should be chewy all the way through.
4
Soak in syrup (15-30 min)
Drain the pearls. Toss in brown sugar syrup (equal parts brown sugar and water, heated until dissolved). Let them soak. This sweetens them and gives that classic dark color.
5
Make the tea & assemble
Brew strong black tea, let it cool. In a glass: boba pearls + syrup on bottom, ice, tea, splash of milk. Stir and enjoy with a wide straw!
Rolling the little balls is basically adult Play-Doh. Way more fun with two people!

🍵 Simple Milk Tea Recipe

For 2 drinks:

  • • 2 cups strong black tea (cooled)
  • • 1/2 cup milk
  • • 2-3 tbsp honey or sugar
  • • Ice
  • • Boba pearls with syrup

Adjust sweetness to taste. Some people like it really sweet!
