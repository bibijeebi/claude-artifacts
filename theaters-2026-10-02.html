Fri Oct 2 · Sat Oct 3 · Wake County

Worth seeing in theaters

No sequels, no prequels, no reboots, no remakes, no capes, no cartoons. Scores pulled Oct 1, 2026.
BLUF

See Primetime at Paragon Fenton, 6:45 Friday. It is the only new release that lands in your lane: A24, original, a documentarian's first narrative feature, and a satire about televised punishment shot like a horror movie.

Runner-up is the Donnie Darko 25th anniversary at Regal Crossroads, 7:00 both nights, which is the better call if To Catch a Predator is not the vibe. Behind those two the slate is thin: the three best-reviewed films that clear your filters are all out of their window locally.

What is playing and worth it

THE PICK

Primetime

Lance Oppenheim · 110 min · A24 · Sept 25 · 2026

Robert Pattinson plays Chris Hansen in the making of To Catch a Predator, shot like a slow-burn arthouse horror film.

87%RT critics180 reviews
79%RT audience
67Metacritic44 critics
BCinemaScore

Why it fitsDead center in the lane. A24, first narrative feature from a documentary stylist, social satire about American appetite for televised punishment. Oppenheim shoots a news-magazine set like a haunted house. The Parasite reference point holds: it is a genre hybrid that keeps changing what it is about. Pattinson is the whole engine and critics are near-unanimous on the performance.

The catchMetacritic 67 and a B CinemaScore say the back half thins out. Several critics think it has nothing to add past the 2025 Predators doc. And the subject is men soliciting minors, so know what the room feels like before you call it date night.

RUNNER-UP

Donnie Darko

Richard Kelly · 113 min theatrical cut · Fathom 4K restoration · Oct 2-4 only · 2001 / 25th anniversary

Suburban teenager, giant rabbit, jet engine, 28 days to the end of the world.

88%RT critics
80%RT audience

Why it fitsNot a remake, not a reboot, the original 2001 cut back on a big screen. Genre hybrid, distinct voice, suburban dread with a soundtrack built for a theater. If the Primetime subject matter kills it as a date, this is the swap. Three Regals, one 7:00 PM show each night.

The catchThree-day Fathom engagement, Oct 2 through 4, then it is gone. Fathom has not said which cut they are running. Twenty-five years old and you have both seen it, so the pitch is the 4K and the room, not discovery. Richard Kelly recorded a new intro that plays before it.

CROWD-PLEASER

Heart of the Beast

David Ayer · 101 min · Lionsgate · Sept 25 · 2026

A retired Special Forces operator and his combat dog survive a seaplane crash and walk 58 miles out of the Alaskan backcountry.

87%RT critics141 reviews
93%RT audience
66Metacritic31 critics
A-CinemaScore

Why it fitsOriginal screenplay, 2017 Black List, no IP attached to it anywhere. A- CinemaScore and a 93 audience number mean it lands with a room. Spartan, one-location, man-and-dog survival, and it respects the military detail rather than cosplaying it.

The catchDavid Ayer is a craftsman, not an auteur. There is no second idea in it. Clears the original-film bar and nothing beyond that.

Walter Thompson-Hernández · 95 min · Rich Spirit · Sept 11 limited, Sept 18 wide · 2026

The catchOnly one evening slot in the whole county, at Crossroads, and the Friday 4:25 is early. Quiet and interior, not a night-out movie.

Clears the filters, not playing here

These four scored higher than most of what is above. They are on the list so you know what you are missing and what to chase on VOD.

Teenage Sex and Death at Camp Miasma

Jane Schoenbrun · 112 min · RT 94% (254) · aud 79% · MC 89

The single best taste match of the whole fall and it is out of its window. A queer filmmaker hired to reboot a slasher franchise becomes fixated on the original's lead actress. Schoenbrun after I Saw the TV Glow, Mubi, Cannes. Nothing in the Triangle. Watch for the Mubi drop.

Your Mother Your Mother Your Mother

Bassam Tariq · 112 min · RT 97% (70) · aud 92% · MC 83

Mahershala Ali as a devout hitman crossing Houston to protect his kids. Highest critic score on the board. Not playing this weekend, but Regal North Hills has a future engagement with pre-orders opening Thursday Oct 15. Put it on the calendar.

Hope

Na Hong-jin · 156 min · RT 78% (162) · aud 77% · MC 67

Na Hong-jin's first film since The Wailing. Wildfires cut off a Korean village, a creature shows up, so does an alien craft. NEON. Opened Sept 9 on 1,500 screens and collapsed to 215. Gone locally.

The Weight

Padraic McKinley · 113 min · RT 94% (124) · aud 83% · MC 70

Ethan Hawke and Russell Crowe, 1933 Oregon, a prisoner smuggling gold through the wilderness for an early release. Original. Vertical gave it a three-week window and it is already out of it.

Ghost in the Cell

Joko Anwar · 106 min · RT 100% (10) · MC n/a

Indonesian supernatural prison horror, opens Oct 2 on a handful of US screens. No connection to Ghost in the Shell. Subtitled, ritual killings, Berlinale premiere. Well Go USA, so no Raleigh date, but it tends to hit VOD fast.
