Locked-in preferences

  • 1. Traditional tobacco only. No infused/flavored. Hard rule.
  • 2. Nic hit matters. More is better within cigar physics. Positive vector.
  • 3. Flavor direction: creamy, cedar, nutty preferred. Pepper fine but not target.
  • 4. Body not the driver. Mild to full all OK.
  • 5. Construction matters. Must light and burn clean.
  • 6. Smoke time ~1 hour or less. Petit corona, corona, short robusto, petit robusto. Robusto borderline. Toro+ too long.
  • 7. Budget: $8-14 per stick at Cigar Barn.
  • 8. Size = smoke time, not ring gauge. Chunky short formats fine.

Vitola reference (smoke time cheat sheet)

  • Petit corona ~4.5"x42 - 30-40 min ✓
  • Corona ~5.5"x42-44 - 45-55 min ✓
  • Short robusto / petit robusto 4-4.5"x50-52 - 35-50 min ✓
  • Robusto 5"x50 - 45-60 min ✓ borderline
  • Toro 6"x50-52 - 60-90 min ✗ too long
  • Churchill 7"x47 - 75-90 min ✗
  • Double corona / Gordo 7"+x54+ - 90+ min ✗

Nic tolerance baseline

Heavy vape/pouch history (Juul menthol, gas station disposables, pouches, gum). Expects higher nic than cigars can physically deliver. Cigar nic = slow mouth absorption, not lung hit. Ceiling is lower than vape by design. Chase flavor AND nic hit together, not one or the other.

Cigar Barn script for Logan

"Traditional tobacco only, no infused. I liked the Fuente Chateau Natural and the Espinosa Habano Toro, preferred the Fuente's flavor but liked the Espinosa's stronger nic hit. Creamy/cedar/nutty is my favorite direction but peppery is fine too. Looking for petit coronas, coronas, short robustos, or petit robustos, anything I can smoke in an hour. Want good nic hit if possible. Budget $8-14."

Target cigars (prioritized)

  • Arturo Fuente Hemingway Short Story - 4"x49 perfecto, ~35-45 min. Same DNA as your Chateau favorite, petit format. ★ top pick.
  • My Father Le Bijou 1922 Petit Robusto - 4.5"x50, ~40-50 min. Pepin Garcia build, nic hit, rich not pepper-bomb.
  • Tatuaje Havana VI Nobles - ~5.25"x46 corona, ~45-55 min. Pepin, Cuban-style, nic-forward, not overly peppery.
  • My Father No. 4 - 4.5"x44 petit corona, ~35-45 min. Creamy Pepin build, short format.
  • Arturo Fuente Don Carlos No. 4 - 4⅜"x44, ~35-45 min. Refined, cedar, nut, petit format, Fuente family.
  • Padron 1964 Anniversary Exclusivo - 5.5"x50 robusto, ~50-60 min. Splurge, creamy/nutty/refined, solid nic.

Avoid / less-priority

  • Anything infused - Tabak, Gurkha Bourbon, Java Maduro, CAO Bourbon, Drew Estate Kentucky Fire Cured. Confirmed dislike.
  • Toro and larger - 60+ min doesn't fit your sit time.
  • Pure pepper bombs - Camacho Triple Maduro, full-ligero Nicaraguans. Not your target profile.
