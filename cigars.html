Locked-in preferences (v4 — size-volume rule)

  • 1. SIZE CAP IS THE PRIMARY RULE. Total nic dose = strength × volume. A full-ligero blend in a 5"x50 robusto is fine. Same blend in a 5.5"x54 La Fuerza or 6"x60 gordo blows past the cap because it has 25-40% more tobacco. Cap: ≤5.25" length, ≤52 ring. Strong blends are FINE inside this envelope.
  • 2. Sweet spot size: 5"x50 robusto. LFD Colorado Oscuro No. 3 nailed it. Papas Fritas (4.5"x44) was too small. Espinosa La Fuerza (5.5"x54) was too big. Full robusto is the target.
  • 3. Flavor direction: Drew Estate maduro / chocolatey / sweet / rich. Papas Fritas profile is the favorite. LFD Colorado Oscuro flavor was also good (mild pepper but rich, sweet, balanced). Pepper-forward fine in moderation.
  • 4. Traditional tobacco only. No infused/flavored. Hard rule.
  • 5. Origin is secondary to size. Earlier Nicaraguan-supremacy theory was wrong — LFD (Dominican) was rated good on rebuy at proper size. Volume cap matters more than origin.
  • 6. Construction matters. All three recent sticks aced this. Table stakes.
  • 7. Smoke time ~1 hour or less. Robusto and smaller. Toro and bigger ruled out by size cap.
  • 8. Budget: $8-16 per stick at Cigar Barn.
  • 9. Ligero/Double Ligero blends NOT excluded. Previously wrongly ruled out. Strong blends in robusto-or-smaller format are the new direction to test.

Retrohale technique (confirmed working)

  • Puff → hold 2-3 seconds → close mouth → gentle exhale through nose
  • Start after the first inch, not immediately on light-up
  • Every 2nd or 3rd puff, not every puff
  • Primary nic delivery mechanism — nasal mucosa absorbs 3-5x more than mouth
  • Also unlocks ~70% of flavor (what you taste as "cedar, chocolate, pepper" lives here)
  • Pepper compounds only detectable via retrohale — if you do not taste pepper with retrohale, you don't taste pepper at all

Vitola reference (smoke time cheat sheet)

  • Petit corona ~4.5"x42 - 30-40 min ✓
  • Corona ~5.5"x42-44 - 45-55 min ✓
  • Short robusto / petit robusto 4-4.5"x50-52 - 35-50 min ✓
  • Robusto 5"x50 - 45-60 min ✓ borderline
  • Toro 6"x50-52 - 60-90 min ✗ too long
  • Churchill 7"x47 - 75-90 min ✗
  • Double corona / Gordo 7"+x54+ - 90+ min ✗

Humidor setup (Jeff's gifted wood humidor)

  • 1. Replace electronic hygrometer battery. Called out as needed.
  • 2. Salt test hygrometer. 1 tsp salt in bottle cap + drops of water (paste), seal in ziplock with hygrometer 6 hours. Should read 75% RH. Adjust offset if not.
  • 3. Target 65-68% RH. Shop humidity is often 70-72%, cigars burn/taste better at 65-68%.
  • 4. Use Boveda 69% packs (2 packs for ~25-count humidor). Set-and-forget for 2-3 months.
  • 5. Rest new cigars 2 weeks before smoking for best flavor and burn.
  • 6. If humidor is new-unused: season 1-2 weeks with 84% Boveda before loading sticks. If Jeff's was already in use, skip seasoning.

Nic tolerance baseline

Heavy vape/pouch history (Juul menthol, gas station disposables, pouches, gum). Cigar nic delivery physically ceiling-limited — slow mouth/nasal absorption vs fast lung absorption in vapes. At best a full-ligero cigar delivers maybe 30-40% of a Juul pod in the same time window. Retrohale technique stacks meaningfully. Chase Nicaraguan ligero + consistent retrohale for max cigar-achievable nic.

Target cigars (size cap: ≤5.25" length, ≤52 ring)

  • ★ LFD Colorado Oscuro No. 3 (5"x50, ~$11) — already confirmed. Buy 2-3 for the humidor. Right size, right profile.
  • ★ Drew Estate Undercrown Maduro Robusto (5"x54, ~$9) — Papas Fritas DNA in robusto format. Slightly chunky ring but right length. Top hunt target.
  • Liga Privada No. 9 Robusto (5"x54, ~$15-17) — actual full-size Papas Fritas blend. Splurge pick.
  • LFD Double Ligero Chiselito (4"x48 chisel, ~$10) — Litto's max-strength blend in petit robusto. Direct test of "small but full-power" theory.
  • My Father Le Bijou 1922 Petit Robusto (4.5"x50, ~$13) — Pepin nic hit, rich not peppery.
  • Padron 1964 Maduro Exclusivo (5.5"x50, ~$15-18) — borderline length, classic robusto ring. Chocolate/cocoa, refined Nicaraguan.
  • 601 Green Label Corona (5"x42, ~$10) — same blend as the La Fuerza Benny found too big, in the proper size. Direct A/B test of volume-not-strength theory.
  • LFD Colorado Oscuro No. 2 (4.25"x48 petit robusto, ~$8) — same blend, shorter format. Backup if No. 3 unavailable.
  • Tatuaje Black Petit Robusto (~5"x50) — Pepin maduro, rich.
  • Aging Room Quattro Nicaragua Maestro (5"x50, ~$10) — exact size match, refined value pick.

Avoid / hard pass

  • Anything over 5.25" length OR over 52 ring. Toros, gordos, churchills, La Fuerza, Trabuco, etc. Volume cap is the hard rule.
  • Infused anything — Tabak, Gurkha Bourbon, M by Macanudo flavored lines, Java Maduro, CAO Bourbon, Drew Estate Kentucky Fire Cured. Confirmed dislike.
  • Connecticut Shade-only mild starter cigars — moved past these, want richer maduro/Habano profiles.
