What Are the Highland Games?

The Grandfather Mountain Highland Games is the premier Scottish cultural festival in the United States. Held annually since 1956, it brings together thousands of kilt-clad Scots and enthusiasts for four days of athletic competitions, music, dancing, and clan gatherings.

Set against the stunning backdrop of the Blue Ridge Mountains, MacRae Meadows transforms into a colorful sea of tartan banners, pipe bands, and centuries-old traditions.

What You'll Experience

Heavy Athletics

Watch brawny athletes compete in ancient tests of strength: caber toss (flipping 20-foot wooden poles), stone put, hammer throw, sheaf toss, and Highland wrestling. Professional and amateur divisions compete throughout the weekend.

Piping & Music

The sound of bagpipes echoing through the mountain valley is unforgettable. See championship pipe bands, solo pipers, fiddlers, and Celtic harpers compete. Friday and Saturday nights feature Celtic rock concerts under the stars.

Highland Dancing

Watch precision dancers perform the Highland Fling, Sword Dance, and other traditional dances. Friday evening features a Scottish Country Dance Gala at Lees-McRae College in Banner Elk.

Day-by-Day Guide

Thursday — Opening & Torchlight Ceremony

The least crowded day. Gates open in late afternoon. Watch the atmospheric torchlight ceremony at twilight where clan representatives announce their families. Perfect for a relaxed introduction. Don't miss the border collie sheep herding demo!

Friday — Full Festival + Celtic Rock

Highland dance competitions begin, amateur heavy athletics, Celtic Grove music performances, and children's caber toss clinic. The day caps off with the Celtic Rock Concert under the stars.

Saturday — THE Main Event ⭐

Professional heavy athletics, championship piping, and the biggest crowds. All the main events happen today. Ends with the Celtic Jam Concert. If you only go one day, make it Saturday.

Sunday — Parade of Tartans & Clan Tug-of-War

The colorful Parade of Tartans, kilted running events, children's events, and the epic Clan Tug-of-War competition. More family-friendly and less crowded than Saturday.

Parking & Getting There

⚠️ No Parking at the Meadows

There is no parking adjacent to the games except after 5 PM on Friday and Saturday. You must use the free remote parking lots and shuttle buses.

Shuttle Info:

  • Roundtrip shuttle: $10/person (cash only, ages 5+)
  • Free parking at Sugar Mountain ski resort and other remote lots
  • Thursday shuttle from Avery County High School: 3:00 PM - 10:30 PM
  • Fri-Sun shuttles: 7:00 AM - 5:00 PM

Address: 2050 Blowing Rock Highway, Linville, NC 28646

Lodging

⚠️ Book NOW — Seriously

Lodging fills up months in advance. Linville and Banner Elk are tiny towns. If you're planning to stay overnight, book immediately. Blowing Rock (15 min away) and Boone (25 min) have more options but also fill fast.

Nearby towns: Blowing Rock, Banner Elk, Boone, Sugar Mountain
