Watch First

Rear Window1954

Hitchcock at his most controlled and most entertaining. One apartment, one man in a wheelchair, escalating dread, and secretly a film about the act of watching itself, which is the whole Hitchcock thesis. Ages the best of anything he made. If you see only one, see this.

Other picks: most shocking and important → Psycho. The critics' "greatest film ever" → Vertigo. But for pure "will I enjoy this tonight," Rear Window wins.
