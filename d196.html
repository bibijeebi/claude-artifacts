The mnemonics, as rebuilt in July

Drug Bust for debits and credits: Debits raise the things you Drive, Eat, and Do (assets, expenses, dividends); credits raise Liabilities, Equity, Revenue. Payday Night for the statement sequence: income statement first, then retained earnings, then balance sheet, then cash flows. Photo vs movie: balance sheet is the photo at a date, the other three are movies for the period. Cover the Van Payment: break-even units = fixed costs / contribution margin per unit; in dollars, fixed costs / CM ratio. Stowaways vs Rent: product costs ride on inventory until sold (DM, DL, overhead, plus freight-in for a merchandiser); period costs are expensed now (selling, admin, store wages, headquarters rent). Pool full of money with a bus driver: ABC pool ÷ expected driver volume = rate, then rate × the product's actual driver usage.

Numbers and formulas that get tested

Ending RE = beginning RE + net income - dividends. Assets = Liabilities + Equity. Horizontal = (current - prior) / prior. Vertical = line / net sales (income statement) or line / total assets (balance sheet). Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities. Debt ratio = liabilities / assets. Debt-to-equity = liabilities / equity. Profit margin = net income / net sales. ROE = net income / equity. Gross margin = sales - COGS. CM = sales - variable costs; CM ratio = CM / sales. Target units = (fixed + target profit) / CM per unit. Margin of safety = actual sales - break-even sales. POHR = estimated overhead / estimated base, applied = rate × actual base. Production = sales + desired ending inventory - beginning inventory. Cash budget: collections in, disbursements out, financing to cover the shortfall. Excel: $ locks a row or column, =(D5-C5)/C5 for change, =C7/C$7 copied down for common-size, formulas with references in every graded cell.
