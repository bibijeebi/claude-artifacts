First solo hunt on North Carolina public game land. Harris Game Land (7-day) accessed via Rex Rd near Cass Holt Rd, Holly Springs. Primary objective was gray squirrel with a .22 LR bolt-action rifle. Secondary: rabbit with Maverick 88 (left in vehicle).

Section 02

Access & Navigation

NC game lands have no formal parking lots, trailheads, or entrance signs. Access is via gated logging roads and roadside pulloffs. This was the single biggest challenge of the operation and consumed approximately one hour of the available daylight window.

Section 05

Terrain & Habitat

The tract transitions from a managed pine plantation near the road to mature mixed hardwoods in the interior. The pine zone is essentially a dead zone for squirrel hunting — no food trees, only pine needles on the ground. The transition zone occurs roughly 200 yards from the gate.

Assessment

What Went Right

  • Located game land access, identified NCWRC gate vs private posted signs
  • Navigated from pine plantation to productive hardwood habitat
  • Found Norris Branch creek bottom — prime future sit spot
  • Legal compliance: blaze orange, valid license, correct firearms, valid hunting day
  • Discovered deer rubs and sassafras for future use
  • All navigation overhead eliminated for future trips — park and hunt in 10 min

What Went Wrong

  • Late start: arrived 1500, lost 1 hour to access confusion. Only 1.5h effective hunting
  • 72°F abnormally warm — squirrels less active in heat, especially mid-afternoon
  • Heavy hunting pressure: subdivision + quarry boundary = easy access for everyone
  • Excessive phone use reset the "quiet clock" repeatedly
  • Did not confirm fresh feeding sign (acorn caps, chewed shells) before committing to sit

Section 09

Lessons Learned

Scout Before You Hunt

Drive access roads on a non-hunting day. Pin gates, pulloffs, and trail entries. Eliminate all navigation overhead so hunt day is 100% hunting.

Morning Hunts Are Mandatory

First light is the primary feeding window. Be in your spot 15 minutes before sunrise, already sitting still. Afternoon hunts are backup, not primary.

Confirm Food Sign Before Sitting

Walk until you find acorn caps, chewed hickory shells, or cut pine cone scales on the ground. Then commit within shooting range of those trees.

Phone Goes Dark

One map check to confirm position, then it's dead until walkout. Every screen check resets the 15-minute quiet window squirrels need to resume activity.

Backyard Beats Pressured Public Land

0.57 acres of unpressured hardwoods in Willow Spring likely holds more squirrels than this game land tract in late February. Know when the backyard is the better option.

Temperature Matters

Hunt cool mornings. 40s–50s produce movement. 72°F in February suppresses activity. Skip hot afternoons unless no other option exists.
