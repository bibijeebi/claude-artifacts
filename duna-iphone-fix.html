Your Phone Will Charge Again

Your phone does not charge because lint blocks the charging port. This is a mechanical problem, not a software problem. A software update will not correct it. You can correct it at home in 10 minutes.

CausePacked lint
FixClean the port
Cost$0–$10

The Problem

Why the phone charges after you blow into the port

Pocket lint goes into the port each day. After many years, the lint becomes a hard plug at the back of the port. The plug stops the cable before the cable is fully in. The metal contacts do not touch. When you blow into the port, the lint moves for a short time. Then the contacts touch, and the phone charges. When the lint moves back, the phone stops charging.

What You Need

You have most of this at home

  • [1]
    A wooden or plastic toothpickThis is the most important tool. It is free.
  • [2]
    A flashlightThe flashlight on a different phone is good.
  • [3]
    Optional: a port cleaning kit ($10, Amazon)The kit has soft picks and brushes. A toothpick does the same work. This kit is also good.
  • [4]
    Optional: compressed air (Amazon)This blows out the small pieces at the end.

Clean The Port

Obey the steps in this sequence

CAUTION

Do not put metal objects into the port. Metal objects cause damage to the contacts on the walls of the port. Do not use pins, paper clips, or SIM tools.

CAUTION

Do not use hot glue in the port. Hot glue can attach to the contacts and cause permanent damage to the port.

  1. Turn the phone off.

  2. Point the flashlight into the port.

    NoteA gray or black wall at the back of the port is lint. This is normal for an old phone.

  3. Put the toothpick into the port. Scrape the back wall with the tip of the toothpick.

    NoteKeep the toothpick away from the side walls. The metal contacts are on the side walls.

  4. Pull the lint out with the toothpick. Do steps 3 and 4 again until you see black plastic at the back.

    NoteA large quantity of gray felt is normal. More lint comes out than you expect.

  5. If you have compressed air, blow short blasts into the port.

  6. Connect the cable and the power adapter. Make sure that the phone shows the charge symbol.

If It Does Not Charge

Two more causes, in sequence

Try a different cable

Old cables have corroded contacts. Examine the flat end of your cable. If one contact is black, the cable is bad. Do a test with a different cable and a different power adapter.

Use a wireless charger

The iPhone X can charge on all Qi wireless chargers (about $12, Amazon). You put the phone on the pad, and it charges. The port is not necessary. This is the correct solution if the port is dead.
