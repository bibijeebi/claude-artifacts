⚠ Critical: Ammunition

These rifles are 70+ years old. Use correct ammo or damage the gas system.

FOR .30-06 GARANDS:

M2 Ball spec · Max 50,000 CUP · 150-174gr bullets only

Lake City surplus is ideal

DO NOT USE:

Commercial hunting loads · Heavy bullets (180gr+) · Max-pressure ammo

.308 Expert Grade: Use 7.62 NATO mil-spec, not commercial .308 Win
