Keith Woods

Keith O'Brien — @KeithWoodsYT
b. ~1993-1996IrelandNationalist Intellectual

Keith Woods (real name Keith O'Brien) is an Irish white nationalist and the current "rising star" of the transatlantic far right, distinguished by his self-conscious intellectualism and rapid trajectory from fringe podcasting to engagement with mainstream figures.

Background

Woods graduated from the National University of Ireland, Galway, embracing far-right politics around 2016 through affiliation with the British National Party. His entry into far-right media came through British Patriotic Alternative leader Mark Collett (2019), then Richard Spencer's "McSpencer Group" podcast alongside "race and IQ" advocate Edward Dutton.

The #BanTheADL Campaign: Woods's profile exploded in September 2023 when he launched the #BanTheADL hashtag on X/Twitter, which went viral. Elon Musk himself liked the original tweet and two follow-up posts, giving an openly antisemitic figure a direct signal boost from the platform's owner. UFC fighter Conor McGregor also retweeted. Woods describes himself as a "raging antisemite" and refers to Jews as enemies of Western civilization.
Intellectual Positioning

Woods presents himself as a baritone-voiced intellectual discussing nationalism through philosophy, identity, and geopolitics. He authored Nationalism: The Politics of Identity and co-hosts the Understanding Platonism podcast with Eric Aarvoll. This philosophical veneer has enabled appearances on increasingly mainstream platforms, including former Trump lawyer Jenna Ellis's radio show on American Family Radio (conservative Christian media).

He has amassed nearly 250,000 followers on X and writes regularly on Substack. Jamestown Foundation analysts identified Woods as the most significant "rising star" in the far right, noting that unlike Spencer, he has maintained consistent ideological positioning while steadily expanding his audience.
